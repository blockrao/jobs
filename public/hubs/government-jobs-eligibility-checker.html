Government Jobs Eligibility: Quick Reference

Before applying for any government job, you need to check eligibility across multiple dimensions: age limit, education qualification, physical requirements, category-wise relaxation, and domicile. This guide covers eligibility for major exams (SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, Police, Defense).

Key Points: Age calculated on exam date (not application date). SC/ST get 5 years age relaxation, OBC get 3 years. No CGPA minimum. Distance degrees are valid. No gap requirement between education milestones.

Category-Wise Relaxation Guidelines

Age Relaxation

  • SC/ST: 5 years additional relaxation above General age limit.
  • OBC: 3 years additional relaxation above General age limit.
  • PwD (Person with Disability): 10 years relaxation (may vary by exam).
  • Ex-Servicemen: Varies by exam. Usually 3 years relaxation + age waiver.
  • War Widow/Widow of Serviceman: No upper age limit (UPSC).

Physical Relaxation

  • Height Relaxation: SC/ST generally get 5 cm relaxation, OBC 2.5 cm (varies by state/exam).
  • Physical Fitness: Reduced standards for SC/ST, OBC in some exams (police, armed forces).
  • PwD: Exemption from physical test in many cases; check specific notification.

Fee Relaxation

  • SC/ST: Zero application fee for all central government exams (SSC, UPSC, RRB, etc.).
  • BPL (Below Poverty Line): Fee waiver (requires certificate).
  • OBC: Generally full fee required (except some state exams).

Common Eligibility Misconceptions

Misconception Reality
Need 60% minimum marks to apply for SSC No minimum percentage/CGPA required. Passing degree is sufficient.
Distance degree not valid for government jobs Valid if from recognized university (IGNOU, DU, etc.). Verify on notification.
Gap in education disqualifies you No gap requirement. Even 10-year gap after graduation is fine.
Age is calculated on application date Age is calculated on exam date (or notification date). You can apply if eligible on exam date.
OBC candidates need creamy layer certificate Yes, OBC Creamy Layer Certificate required from authorized authority.
Married women can't apply for police No such restriction. Marital status doesn't matter (except defense cadre; check notification).

Documents Required for Government Job Application

Essential Documents

  • Aadhaar Card or Voter ID (for identity)
  • Bachelor's/10th/12th Certificate (as per exam requirement)
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) — from authorized authority, not older than 1 year
  • PwD Certificate (if applicable) — issued by civil hospital or authorized doctor
  • Date of Birth Proof (Birth Certificate or 10th Certificate)

Additional Documents

  • Character Certificate (from school/college principal)
  • Medical Fitness Certificate (for physical posts like police/military)
  • Domicile Certificate (state-specific exams)
  • Employment Exchange Registration (for some state exams)

How to Check Eligibility Before Applying

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Download notification: Get official notification from SSC.gov.in, RBI.org.in, UPSC.gov.in, etc.
  2. Check age: Calculate age on exam date (not application date).
  3. Check education: Verify your degree matches required qualification.
  4. Check category: Identify if you're General/OBC/SC/ST. Apply for correct category.
  5. Check documents: Gather certificates (caste, DOB, education).
  6. Apply: Fill form carefully. Double-check age, name, education, category.
  7. Download admit card: Download 1 month before exam date.
