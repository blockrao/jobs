1. SSC Overview

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is a central government recruitment agency responsible for recruiting staff for various ministries and departments. SSC conducts multiple competitive exams every year to fill vacancies for Group B and Group C positions across the country. With 2,347+ posts available this year, SSC offers the largest volume of government job opportunities in India.

2. Exam Types: CGL, CHSL, CPO, Stenographer, MTS

SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level): 2,347 posts, Bachelor's degree required, ₹25K–₹35K entry salary. Includes Assistant, Statistician, Inspector roles. Success rate: 0.3%.

SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level): 456 posts, 12th pass required, ₹21K–₹30K entry. Postal Assistant, Clerk roles.

SSC CPO (Central Police Organization): 156 posts, Bachelor's degree, ₹35K–₹45K entry, Physical test required for police roles.

SSC Stenographer: 234 posts, 12th pass, shorthand typing skill required, ₹21K–₹30K.

SSC MTS: 89 posts, 10th pass, ₹18K–₹25K entry, easiest SSC exam.

4. Eligibility Requirements

  • Nationality: Indian citizen
  • Age: 18–32 years for SSC CGL (with relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PWD up to 37–42 years)
  • Education: Bachelor's degree for CGL, 12th pass for CHSL, 10th for MTS
  • Character: No criminal record required
  • Physical Fitness: Medical fitness certificate

5. Selection Process

Tier 1 (Preliminary): 60-minute online MCQ test, 200 marks, qualifying round only.

Tier 2 (Main): 200 marks, main exam that counts toward final merit list.

Tier 3 (Descriptive): 100 marks, essay/application writing (for CGL only).

Tier 4 (Skill Test): Typing test for Data Entry Operator, Stenographer roles.

Document Verification: Final step after qualifying all tiers.

6. FAQ

Q: What's the success rate in SSC?
A: SSC CGL has 0.3% success rate (1 out of 333 applicants selected). This makes it highly competitive but with better odds than UPSC (0.08%).

Q: How long to prepare for SSC?
A: Typical preparation: 6–12 months for CGL. Daily 4–6 hours of study recommended.

Q: Can I apply for multiple SSC exams?
A: Yes! No restriction. Many apply for CGL, CHSL, and CPO simultaneously.

Q: What's pension in SSC jobs?
A: 50% of last salary. SSC officer retiring with ₹70K salary = ₹35K/month pension for life.
