Madhya Pradesh Government Jobs Overview

Madhya Pradesh, India's geographical center state, offers 2,500+ government job vacancies annually through MPPSC (state services), police, education, health, and administrative roles. Entry salaries range ₹28K–₹40K with career ceilings exceeding ₹110K. MP maintains organized recruitment with moderate competition and balanced hiring timelines.

MP Jobs Key Facts: Entry ₹28K–₹40K. Selection rate 1–2%. 2,500+ vacancies yearly. Prep 8–14 months. Lifetime pension 50%. Moderate hiring speed (12–18 months).

MPPSC Exam Details

  • Vacancies: 600+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹45K–₹50K
  • Difficulty: Similar to SSC CGL (1–2% success)
  • Exam Structure: Prelims → Mains → Interview
  • Preparation: 12–18 months
  • Career Ceiling: ₹110K–₹130K as Chief Secretary

Madhya Pradesh Police Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 1,000+ constable + 150+ SI yearly
  • Entry Salary: Constable ₹21K–₹25K, SI ₹35K–₹40K
  • Selection: Written + Physical + Interview
  • Physical Standards: 165cm (male), 152cm (female), 5km in 25 min
  • Career Ceiling: Commissioner (₹110K+)

Madhya Pradesh Teacher Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 600+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹28K–₹35K
  • Exam: CTET + MP TET + written
  • Benefits: 120+ annual leave, pension 50%, security
  • Prep Time: 6–12 months
