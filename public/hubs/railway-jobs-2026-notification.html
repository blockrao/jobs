Railway Jobs Eligibility Criteria

1. RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories)

  • Age: 18–30 (General), 18–33 (OBC), 18–35 (SC/ST).
  • Education: Bachelor's degree in any stream.
  • CGPA/Percentage: No minimum (just degree required).
  • Eligibility: Indian citizen, no medical disqualification.

2. RRB Group D

  • Age: 18–28 (General), 18–30 (OBC), 18–32 (SC/ST).
  • Education: 10th pass or 12th pass (varies by post).
  • Height: 160 cm (Male, General), 150 cm (Female). SC/ST: 5 cm relaxation.
  • Physical Fitness: Medical examination mandatory.

3. RRB Loco Pilot

  • Age: 18–28 (General), 18–30 (OBC), 18–32 (SC/ST).
  • Education: 10th pass + ITI certification in relevant trades.
  • Vision: 6/6 (correctable to 6/6) mandatory.
  • Medical: Stringent medical examination.

Railway Job Salary & Career Progression

RRB NTPC Level 2 Career Path

  • Entry Salary: ₹35K–₹40K (with HRA, allowances).
  • After 5 years: ₹50K–₹60K (promotion to Senior Level 2).
  • After 10 years: ₹65K–₹80K (promotion to Level 3).
  • After 20 years: ₹100K–₹120K (Senior management role).
  • Lifetime Pension: 50% of last-drawn salary.

RRB Group D Career Path

  • Entry Salary: ₹25K–₹30K.
  • After 10 years: ₹40K–₹50K (promotion to assistant role).
  • Career ceiling: ₹60K–₹80K (senior position).
  • Lifetime Pension: 50% of last-drawn salary.

How to Prepare for Railway Jobs 2026

Preparation Timeline (6 months)

Month 1–2: Foundation Building

  • Quantitative Aptitude: Number system, Percentage, Ratio, Average, SI/CI (Maths fundamentals).
  • Reasoning: Analogy, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding.
  • General Knowledge: Static GK (History, Geography, Science), Current Affairs (Indian railways).
  • Daily study: 2 hours Quant, 1.5 hours Reasoning, 1.5 hours GK.

Month 3–4: Topic Mastery

  • Complete all topics: Quant (Geometry, Trigonometry, Time-Work), Reasoning (all types).
  • GK depth: Railway-specific knowledge (Indian railway history, rail zones, stations).
  • Daily practice: 100 Quant questions, 50 Reasoning questions, 50 GK questions.

Month 5–6: Mock Tests & Speed

  • Take 30–40 full-length mock tests.
  • Analyze each mock: Time management, accuracy, weak topics.
  • Focus on speed + accuracy: Complete 100 Quant in 30 minutes, 100 Reasoning in 20 minutes.
