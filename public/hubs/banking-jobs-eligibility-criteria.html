Banking Jobs Eligibility: Complete Criteria 2026

Banking jobs (IBPS, SBI, RBI) are among India's most sought-after government careers. Entry salary ₹20K–₹50K depending on post, lifetime job security, and pension. This guide covers detailed eligibility criteria for all major banking exams, including age limits, education requirements, category-wise relaxation, and application fees.

Key Facts: Age 18–37 (depending on exam & category). Education: 10th/12th or Bachelor's (depends on post). No minimum CGPA/percentage. Application fees ₹0–₹600. SC/ST get fee waiver. Unlimited attempts until age limit.

Education Requirements Clarified

What Degrees are Eligible?

  • Any stream bachelor's: B.Sc, BCom, BA, BCA, BTech, BBA, etc.
  • Distance degrees: Valid (IGNOU, DU, etc.) if recognized university.
  • Professional degrees: CA, CS eligible (treat as bachelor's degree).
  • 12th pass candidates: Eligible for IBPS Clerk, SBI Clerk.
  • 10th pass candidates: Some banks accept for Clerk (check notification).

What About CGPA/Percentage?

  • No minimum CGPA/percentage required.
  • Even 40% passing degree qualifies for IBPS/SBI.
  • Only requirement: Degree from recognized university.
  • No education stream restriction (engineering, commerce, science, arts all equal).

Category-Wise Relaxation

Age Relaxation

  • SC/ST: 5 years age relaxation above General limit.
  • OBC: 3 years age relaxation above General limit. But OBC Creamy Layer Certificate required (not more than 1 year old).
  • PwD: 10 years relaxation (varies by bank).
  • Ex-Servicemen: 3–5 years relaxation (military experience)

Fee Relaxation

  • SC/ST: Zero application fee for all IBPS and SBI exams.
  • General/OBC: Full fee (₹450–₹600) required.
  • BPL: Fee waiver with BPL certificate (if applicable).

Application Requirements Checklist

Documents Required

  • ☐ Aadhaar Card (for identity verification)
  • ☐ Bachelor's/12th/10th Certificate (education proof)
  • ☐ Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) — not older than 1 year
  • ☐ OBC Creamy Layer Certificate (OBC candidates only)
  • ☐ PwD Certificate (if applicable)
  • ☐ Character Certificate (from school/college)
  • ☐ Address proof (electricity bill, gas bill, etc.)
  • ☐ Photograph (passport size, recent, colored)

Banking Job Age Calculation

  • Age calculated on: Registration date (IBPS) or Application closing date (SBI).
  • Example: If IBPS notification on 1 Jan, closing on 20 Jan → age calculated on 20 Jan.
  • Birth date: As per 10th certificate or Aadhaar. Must match across documents.
  • Age waiver: None. Age must match exactly on document and application date.

Common Eligibility Myths

Myth Reality
Need 50% CGPA for banking jobs No. 40% passing degree is sufficient. No minimum CGPA.
Distance degree not valid Valid if from recognized university (IGNOU, DU, etc.).
Engineering candidates preferred No preference. Any stream equal. Commerce/Arts/Science same.
Age is calculated today No. Age calculated on exam date or application closing date.
OBC candidates don't need creamy layer certificate False. OBC creamy layer certificate mandatory for IBPS/SBI.
Married women can't apply False. Marital status doesn't matter. Women equally eligible.
Education gap disqualifies No. Gap between degree + job doesn't matter.
