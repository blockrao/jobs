Government Jobs for Women 2026

Government jobs offer exceptional opportunities for women with reserved quota (10–33%), physical relaxation, and equal salary/pension. This guide covers women's quota, age benefits, and best exams for women.

Key Facts: 10–33% women quota across exams. Women compete only with women. Physical height requirements relaxed (150 cm vs 160 cm male). Same salary, promotion, pension as men. No gender-based age relaxation.

Physical Requirements for Women (Relaxed)

  • Police Constable: Height 150 cm (vs 160 cm male). 10 cm relaxation.
  • Railway: Height 150 cm (vs 160 cm male).
  • Physical test: Women run 100m in 18 sec (vs 16 sec male).
  • Medical: Same stringency as men (no laxity).
