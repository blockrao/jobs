NEET vs Government Doctor: Which Path Pays More?

NEET (National Eligibility Test): Pass NEET → Medical college (₹5L–₹10L costs) → Doctor job. Entry salary ₹25K–₹30K (government hospital). Private practice = ₹50K–₹100K+ potential. GOVERNMENT MBBS DOCTOR: Entry ₹25K–₹30K (same), but job security + pension. 5-year government career = ₹15L earnings + ₹10L pension = ₹25L total. Private practice (uncertain) = ₹20L–₹50L. Verdict: NEET into government = safest path to ₹1Cr+ lifetime earnings.

State Health Officer Jobs: Nursing vs Paramedic Comparison

ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife): Entry ₹20K–₹25K. Vacancies: 5K+ yearly. Exam difficulty: Low. Career ceiling: ₹80K. GNM (General Nursing): Entry ₹23K–₹28K. Vacancies: 3K+ yearly. Difficulty: Low. Ceiling: ₹90K. Paramedic: Entry ₹22K–₹27K. Vacancies: 2K+ yearly. Difficulty: Medium. Ceiling: ₹85K. Best pick: ANM (most vacancies + lowest difficulty = fastest hire in 2–3 weeks).

Fastest Government Health Job for 12th Pass (Non-NEET Path)

You don't need NEET to work in health sector. ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist): Entry ₹3K–₹5K (lowest). Vacancies: 50K+. Direct hire (no exam). ANM: Entry ₹20K–₹25K. Vacancies: 5K+. Simple exam (basic science). Paramedic: Entry ₹22K–₹27K. Vacancies: 2K+. Moderate exam. Fastest to government health job: ASHA (immediate hiring, no exam) or ANM (2–3 months total timeline).
