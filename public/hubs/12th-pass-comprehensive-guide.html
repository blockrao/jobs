SSC CHSL (10+2 Level)

  • Vacancies: 5,000+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹21K–₹25K
  • Roles: Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Data Entry Operator (DEO)
  • Selection: Written (Tier 1 + Tier 2) + Typing Test
  • Exam Difficulty: Moderate (1–2% success rate)
  • Preparation: 6–12 months
  • Career Ceiling: ₹70K–₹100K as senior clerk/inspector

Bank Clerk (IBPS)

  • Vacancies: 7,000+ yearly across all banks
  • Entry Salary: ₹18K–₹25K
  • Selection: Prelims → Mains → Group Discussion + Interview
  • Exam Difficulty: Moderate (1–2% success rate)
  • Preparation: 4–6 months
  • Career Path: Clerk → PO (₹45K) → Manager (₹75K+)
  • Benefits: Bonus structure, medical, pension 50%

Railway Clerk (RRB NTPC)

  • Vacancies: 3,000+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹18K–₹22K
  • Roles: Station Master, Traffic Assistant, Clerk
  • Selection: Written (easy) + Interview
  • Exam Difficulty: Easy–Moderate (2–4% success rate)
  • Preparation: 4–6 months
  • Career Path: Clerk → Station Master → Chief Station Master (₹80K+)

Police Constable

  • Vacancies: 15,000+ yearly (HIGHEST vacancies)
  • Entry Salary: ₹21K–₹25K
  • Selection: Written + Physical Test (5km run, pullups) + Interview
  • Physical Standards: 165cm (male), 152cm (female), 20 pullups
  • Preparation: 4–6 months (focus on physical fitness)
  • Career Path: Constable → Sub-Inspector (₹40K) → Inspector (₹60K+)

Postal Jobs (Mail Guard/Postman)

  • Vacancies: 3,000+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹18K–₹22K (easiest entry)
  • Selection: Written + Physical + Interview
  • Physical Standards: Basic fitness (lower than police)
  • Preparation: 4–6 months
  • Career Path: Mail Guard → Postman → Postal Supervisor (₹60K+)

12th Pass Exam Preparation Strategy

  • Written Exam: General Awareness, English, Math, Reasoning (10+2 standard)
  • Key Focus: Time management (test-taking speed), accuracy over complex concepts
  • Physical (for Police): Daily running, strength training, flexibility
  • Typing Test (for CHSL/Clerk): 30+ WPM in English or Hindi
  • Best Approach: Mock tests weekly, solve 2,000+ previous year questions
