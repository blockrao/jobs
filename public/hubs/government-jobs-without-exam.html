Government Jobs Without Exam: Direct Recruitment & Walk-Ins

Some government jobs don't require written exams. Direct recruitment (ASHA, Anganwadi), walk-in interviews (nurse, lab tech), and contract roles offer quick entry without competitive exams. Salary ₹15K–₹25K. Not permanent, but renewable contracts.

Key Facts: No written exam required. Direct interview or merit-based selection. Eligible: 10th/12th pass. Salary ₹15K–₹25K. Contract-based (not permanent). Apply: Direct to schools, hospitals, municipal offices.

How to Apply for Jobs Without Exam

  • Find announcements: Check school/hospital/municipal office notice boards.
  • Direct walk-in: Show up on interview date with documents (10th certificate, ID).
  • Eligibility check: Ensure age/education matches (no exam, but basics checked).
  • Interview: 10–20 min personal interview (motivation, experience, communication).
  • Selection: Merit-based on interview + certificate check.
