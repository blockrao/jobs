State Police Recruitment 2026: Complete Guide

State police recruitment is one of India's largest government recruitment drives, offering 50,000–80,000 vacancies annually across all states. Police constable is the entry-level position with salary ₹25K–₹30K (varies by state). This guide covers all 28 states, eligibility, salary, exam pattern, and preparation strategy.

Key Facts: 50K–80K vacancies across states 2026. Age 18–23 (General), 18–28 (SC/ST). Height 160 cm (male), 150 cm (female) with relaxation for SC/ST. Salary ₹25K–₹30K entry. Each state runs separate recruitment.

Police Constable Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

  • General: 18–23 years (most states), 18–25 (some states like Maharashtra, Karnataka).
  • OBC: 18–26 years (most states).
  • SC/ST: 18–28 years (most states).
  • Exception: Check specific state notification. Some states give no upper age limit for SC/ST.

Height Requirements

  • Male (General): 160 cm (minimum) to 175 cm (some states vary).
  • Female (General): 150 cm (minimum) to 163 cm.
  • SC/ST: 5 cm relaxation (male 160 cm → 155 cm acceptable).
  • OBC: 2.5 cm relaxation (varies by state).

Physical Requirements

  • Chest (Male): 77 cm (average), 82 cm (expanded).
  • Eyesight: 6/6 (correctable to 6/6) or 6/9 (varies by state).
  • Medical fitness: Mandatory examination. Must pass running, push-ups, long jump.

Education

  • 10th or 12th pass (depending on state).
  • No minimum percentage required.
  • Distance education degree also valid.

How to Prepare for Police Constable Exam

Written Exam Preparation (3–6 months)

  • Quantitative Aptitude (25%): Percentage, Average, Ratio, SI/CI, Time-Work. 1 hour daily.
  • Reasoning (25%): Analogy, Series, Coding, Direction, Blood Relation. 1 hour daily.
  • General Knowledge (25%): Static (History, Geography, Science) + Current Affairs. 1 hour daily.
  • English (25%): Spotting errors, reading comprehension, sentence correction. 1 hour daily.
  • Daily study: 4–5 hours. Take 20–30 mock tests.

Physical Preparation (3–6 months parallel)

  • Running (100m): Target sub-16 sec (male), sub-18 sec (female). Practice on track 3x weekly.
  • Long Jump: Target 4+ meters. Practice technique + strength. 2x weekly.
  • High Jump: Target 1.2m. Requires flexibility + explosive power. 1x weekly.
  • General fitness: Push-ups, sit-ups, pull-ups. 30 min daily.

Interview Preparation (1–2 months)

  • Personal motivation: Why police? Clear answer (social service, security interest, family background).
  • Policing knowledge: Recent police reforms, crime trends, community policing.
  • Current affairs: Know 20–30 major current events (politics, economy, crimes).
  • Communication: Speak clearly, maintain confidence, honesty valued.

Best Resources for Police Exam Prep

  • Books: RS Aggarwal (Quant), Arun Sharma (Reasoning), Lucent GK (Current Affairs).
  • Online: Unacademy, PW, Adda247 (free + paid courses).
  • Mock Tests: Adda247, Testbook (police-specific mocks).
