Police Jobs India Overview

Police jobs in India offer 50,000+ vacancies annually across constable, sub-inspector, and inspector levels. Entry salaries range ₹21K–₹50K with career ceilings exceeding ₹100K. Police recruitment is nationwide, action-oriented, and accessible with 6–18 month preparation depending on position level.

Police Jobs Key Facts: Entry ₹21K–₹50K (varies by rank). Selection rate 1–3%. 50,000+ vacancies yearly. Prep 6–18 months. Lifetime pension 50% + lifetime benefits. Physical fitness essential.

Police Constable Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 30,000+ yearly across all states
  • Entry Salary: ₹21K–₹25K
  • Selection Process: Written exam + Physical test + Interview
  • Physical Standards: 165cm (male), 152cm (female), 5km run in 25 min, 20 pullups (male)
  • Preparation: 6–12 months (easiest police rank)
  • Career Ceiling: ₹50K–₹60K as senior constable/inspector

Police Sub-Inspector Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 8,000+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹35K–₹40K
  • Selection Process: Written (harder than constable) + Physical + Interview
  • Physical Standards: Similar to constable (165cm, 5km run)
  • Preparation: 12–18 months (moderate difficulty)
  • Career Ceiling: ₹80K–₹90K as inspector/superintendent

Police Inspector Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 1,000+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹50K–₹60K (highest entry among police ranks)
  • Selection Process: Written (SSC CGL level) + Physical + Interview
  • Exam Difficulty: Similar to SSC CGL (1–2% success rate)
  • Preparation: 18–24 months
  • Career Ceiling: ₹100K–₹120K as Commissioner

Police Benefits

  • Pension: 50% lifetime pension
  • Free Travel: Free train/bus for self + 4 family members
  • Medical: Full medical insurance + family coverage
  • Leave: 40+ annual leave + 20 sick leave
  • Group Insurance: ₹10L–₹20L group insurance + disability coverage
  • Housing: Government housing assistance (state-dependent)

Police Exam Preparation

  • Constable: GS (history, geography, polity), current affairs, maths, reasoning. Focus: Physical fitness training (running, strength).
  • Sub-Inspector: Similar to SSC CHSL (10+2 level). Added: Law & Constitution, state affairs.
  • Inspector: Similar to SSC CGL. Added: Judgment writing, leadership skills, administrative knowledge.
