UPSC IAS Overview

UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) IAS (Indian Administrative Service) is India's toughest and most prestigious civil service exam. Entry salary ₹56K–₹75K with career ceiling exceeding ₹200K as Cabinet Secretary. Only 200–350 positions yearly for 2M+ applicants (0.08% selection).

UPSC Key Facts: Entry ₹56K–₹75K (highest entry-level govt). Selection 0.08% (1 from 1,200+). 200–350 vacancies yearly. Prep 12–24 months minimum. Lifetime pension 50% + ₹15L gratuity. Ultimate prestige + power + authority.

UPSC Selection Rate

  • Total Applications: 2M+ yearly (1–1.2M prelims appears)
  • Prelims Cutoff: Top 8,000–10,000 (0.5%) qualify for mains
  • Mains Cutoff: Top 1,000–1,200 (0.05%) qualify for interview
  • Final Selection: 200–350 (0.08%) selected
  • IAS Positions: 150 yearly (0.005% of applicants)
  • IPS Positions: 150 yearly (0.005% of applicants)

UPSC Preparation Timeline

  • Months 1–4: Fundamentals (NCERT 6–12, basic history, geography)
  • Months 5–10: Core subjects (polity, economics, science, current affairs)
  • Months 11–16: Optional subject + answer writing practice
  • Months 17–24: Revision + mains practice + interview prep
  • Full-time approach: 8–12 hours daily study = 18–24 months success
  • Part-time: 4–6 hours daily = 24–36 months minimum

UPSC Career Path

  • IAS Entry: ₹56K–₹75K as Assistant Secretary/Commissioner
  • Year 10: ₹90K as Joint Secretary
  • Year 20: ₹100K+ as Additional Secretary
  • Year 30: ₹120K as Chief Secretary (state) or Cabinet Secretary (national)
  • Career Ceiling: ₹200K–₹250K as Cabinet Secretary (India's top bureaucrat)
  • Pension: 50% of final salary (₹60K–₹100K/month for life)

Why UPSC is Hardest

  • Generalist Knowledge: Requires mastery across 20+ subjects (history, geography, economy, science, law, ethics, current affairs)
  • Depth: 8–10 years of subject mastery needed for consistent success
  • Answer Writing: Mains demands essay + analytical skills (not just knowledge)
  • Personality: Interview tests temperament, judgment, leadership potential (subjective)
  • Rejection Rate: 99.92% fail (2M applicants, 200 selected)
  • Attempt Limit: 6 attempts (only 1–2 succeed per attempt batch)
