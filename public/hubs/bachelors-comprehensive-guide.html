SSC CGL Exam

  • Vacancies: 8,000+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹31K–₹45K
  • Difficulty: Hard (0.1–1% success rate)
  • Exam Structure: Tier 1 (180 min) → Tier 2 (120 min, two papers) → Interview (100 marks)
  • Preparation: 12–18 months
  • Career Ceiling: ₹120K–₹150K as ministry secretary

Banking PO & Clerk

  • PO Vacancies: 8,000+ yearly
  • PO Entry Salary: ₹40K–₹50K (highest among entry-level)
  • Clerk Vacancies: 7,000+ yearly
  • Clerk Entry Salary: ₹25K–₹30K
  • Selection: Prelims → Mains → Group Discussion + Interview
  • Preparation: 6–12 months (PO), 4–6 months (Clerk)
  • Career Path: Clerk → PO → Manager → Senior Manager (₹80K+)

Teacher (CTET)

  • Vacancies: 3,000+ yearly (primary + secondary)
  • Entry Salary: ₹28K–₹35K
  • Selection: CTET + State TET + Written
  • Benefits: 120+ annual leave, 50% pension, lifetime security
  • Preparation: 6–12 months
  • Career Ceiling: ₹70K–₹100K as principal/educator

IT-Tech Sector Jobs

  • Vacancies: 2,000+ yearly (NIC, C-DAC, DRDO, ISRO)
  • Entry Salary: ₹30K–₹40K
  • Exam: GATE (for some), SSC CGL (for others)
  • Disciplines: Software development, data science, networking
  • Preparation: 12–18 months (requires programming skills)
  • Career Ceiling: ₹100K+ as chief scientist/architect

Which Bachelor Degree Pays Most in Government Jobs?

B.Tech (Engineering): Entry ₹40K–₹50K (Central PSUs, Railways). Highest ceiling ₹200K+. B.Sc (Science): Entry ₹30K–₹40K (Research, Health). Ceiling ₹150K. B.Com (Commerce): Entry ₹30K–₹35K (Banking, Finance). Ceiling ₹120K. B.A (Arts): Entry ₹28K–₹35K (Admin, Education). Ceiling ₹100K. **Winner:** B.Tech earns 30–40% more over career.

SSC vs Banking vs UPSC: Bachelor Degree Comparison

SSC (≥ 10+2 OR degree): Entry ₹25K–₹30K, 5K vacancies, 2–5% selection. Banking PO (degree required): Entry ₹30K–₹35K, 4K vacancies, 3–5% selection. UPSC (degree required): Entry ₹56K–₹90K, 500+ vacancies, 0.1–0.5% selection. **Smart move:** Bachelor + UPSC = highest salary + prestige.

Fastest Job for Bachelor Graduates (3-Month Timeline)

Not UPSC (12+ months). Try: Railway RRB (Graduate recruitment) - 3K vacancies, ₹28K–₹32K entry, 2–3 months timeline. CDS (Combined Defense Service) - 1K vacancies, ₹34K–₹45K entry (officer rank). SSC CGL - 1K vacancies, ₹27K–₹32K entry, 4–5 months timeline. **Fastest:** Railway RRB (start to join in 4–5 months).
