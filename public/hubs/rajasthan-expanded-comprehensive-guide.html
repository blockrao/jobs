Rajasthan Government Jobs Overview

Rajasthan, India's largest state by area, offers 3,000+ government job vacancies annually through RPSC RAS (state civil services), police, education, health, and administrative roles. Entry salaries range ₹28K–₹45K with career ceilings exceeding ₹120K. Rajasthan jobs are ideal for candidates seeking state-level administration with moderate competition and reasonable hiring timelines.

Rajasthan Jobs Key Facts: Entry ₹28K–₹45K. Selection rate 1–2%. 3,000+ vacancies yearly. Prep 6–12 months. Lifetime pension 50%. Moderate hiring speed (12–18 months).

RPSC RAS Exam Details

  • Vacancies: 600+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹48K–₹55K
  • Difficulty: Similar to SSC CGL (1–2% success rate)
  • Exam Structure: Prelims → Mains → Interview
  • Preparation: 12–18 months
  • Career Ceiling: ₹120K–₹140K as Chief Secretary

Rajasthan Police Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 1,200+ constable + 150+ SI yearly
  • Entry Salary: Constable ₹21K–₹25K, SI ₹35K–₹40K
  • Selection: Written + Physical + Interview
  • Physical Standards: 165cm (male), 152cm (female), 5km in 25 min
  • Career Ceiling: Commissioner (₹120K+)

Rajasthan Teacher Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 800+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹28K–₹35K
  • Exam: CTET + Rajasthan TET + written exam
  • Benefits: 120+ annual leave, pension 50%, job security
  • Prep Time: 6–12 months
