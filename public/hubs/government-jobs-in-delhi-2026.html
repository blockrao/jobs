Best Delhi Government Jobs by Salary

1. Delhi Police Constable (2,000–3,000 vacancies)

  • Salary: ₹25K–₹30K entry.
  • Age: 18–25 (General), 18–28 (SC/ST).
  • Selection: Written exam + physical test + interview.

2. Delhi School Teacher (1,000+ vacancies)

  • Salary: ₹25K–₹50K (depends on grade/experience).
  • Education: Bachelor's + B.Ed.
  • Selection: CTET + interview + document verification.

3. DSSSB Clerk/Patwari (1,000+ vacancies)

  • Salary: ₹25K–₹35K.
  • Education: 12th pass.
  • Selection: Online exam (reasoning, quant, GK).

How to Apply for Delhi Government Jobs

  1. Visit official website (SSSB, DSSSB, Police, School board).
  2. Check notification: Eligibility, vacancies, exam date.
  3. Register online: Email, mobile, date of birth.
  4. Fill application form: Personal details, education, category.
  5. Upload documents: 10th, 12th, degree, photograph, signature.
  6. Pay fee: Online payment (credit/debit card, net banking).
  7. Download confirmation & admit card.
