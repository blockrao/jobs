Delhi Government Jobs Overview

Delhi, India's capital, offers 5,000+ government job vacancies annually through UPSC (central services), DSSSB (state services), police, education, and health sectors. Entry salaries range ₹25K–₹40K with career ceilings exceeding ₹150K. Delhi maintains India's most competitive government recruitment system with transparent processes and fast hiring timelines.

Delhi Jobs Key Facts: Entry ₹25K–₹40K. Selection rate 0.5–2% (highly competitive). 5,000+ vacancies yearly. Prep 6–18 months. Lifetime pension 50%. Fast hiring (12–18 months).

UPSC Exam: India's Most Prestigious Service

  • Vacancies: 500+ yearly (IAS, IPS, IFS combined)
  • Entry Salary: ₹56K–₹90K (highest among Indian exams)
  • Difficulty: World's hardest exam (0.1–0.5% success rate)
  • Exam Structure: Prelims → Mains → Interview
  • Preparation: 12–24 months typical
  • Career Ceiling: ₹150K–₹200K+ as Cabinet Secretary

DSSSB Recruitment: Delhi's Main Employer

  • Vacancies: 2,000+ yearly (teachers, supervisors, clerks)
  • Entry Salary: ₹25K–₹35K depending on role
  • Selection: Written exam only
  • Difficulty: Moderate (1–3% success rate)
  • Prep Time: 6–12 months
  • Career Ceiling: ₹80K–₹100K as principal/supervisor

Delhi Police Recruitment

  • Vacancies: 800+ sub-inspector + 1,000+ constable yearly
  • Entry Salary: Constable ₹21K–₹25K, Sub-Inspector ₹35K–₹42K
  • Selection: Written + Physical + Interview
  • Physical Standards: 165cm (male), 152cm (female), 5km in 25 min
  • Career Ceiling: Commissioner (₹120K+)

Delhi Teacher Recruitment (DSSSB)

  • Vacancies: 1,500+ yearly
  • Entry Salary: ₹30K–₹35K
  • Exam: CTET + Delhi TET + written
  • Benefits: 120+ annual leave, pension 50%, job security
  • Prep Time: 6–12 months
