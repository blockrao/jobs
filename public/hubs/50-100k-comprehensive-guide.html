UPSC IAS (₹56K–₹75K) — INDIA'S HIGHEST

  • Entry Salary: ₹56K–₹75K (India's second-highest entry-level)
  • Vacancies: 200+ yearly
  • Career Ceiling: ₹200K+ as Cabinet Secretary
  • Exam: UPSC CSE (Prelims → Mains → Interview)
  • Difficulty: Hardest in India (0.08% success rate)
  • Prep Time: 12–24 months (minimum)
  • Benefits: Authority, power, international exposure, ₹15L+ gratuity
  • Best For: Ambitious candidates wanting to shape India's future

UPSC IPS (₹56K–₹75K) — POLICE SERVICE

  • Entry Salary: ₹56K–₹75K (same as IAS)
  • Vacancies: 150+ yearly
  • Career Ceiling: ₹150K–₹200K as Police Commissioner
  • Role: State police chief, paramilitary, CBI/NIA command
  • Selection: UPSC exam + SSB Interview + Medical
  • Prep Time: 12–24 months
  • Best For: Action-oriented candidates with leadership aspirations

University Professor (₹45K–₹60K)

Scientist (CSIR/ICMR/ICAR) (₹47K–₹65K)

  • Entry Salary: ₹47K–₹65K (varies by institute)
  • Vacancies: 1,000+ yearly

  • Exam: NET/GATE + Interview
  • Difficulty: Hard (1–2% success)

Customs Inspector (₹50K–₹65K)

Banking PO (IBPS): ₹40K–₹45K entry. Selection rate 3–5%. Ceiling: ₹100K–₹120K. **Best ROI:** UPSC (hardest but highest lifetime earnings).

UPSC IAS vs Banking PO: ₹50K Entry Jobs Compared

IAS: ₹56K–₹90K entry. Power + prestige + growth. 20-year salary = ₹3Cr+. Banking PO: ₹40K–₹45K entry. Stable + secure. 20-year salary = ₹2Cr. **Difference:** IAS earns ₹50L more lifetime but harder to get (0.1–0.5% vs 3–5%).
