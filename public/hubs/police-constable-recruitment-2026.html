Police Constable Recruitment 2026 Overview

Police constable is India's largest government recruitment — 15,000+ vacancies yearly nationwide. Entry salary Rs. 21,000–Rs. 25,000 with lifetime pension, job security, and clear 25-year career path to Senior Inspector (Rs. 85,000+). Selection rate 0.8–1.5% (competitive but achievable with discipline and fitness). Physical fitness is primary filter — 5km run and 20 pullups are make-or-break requirements.

Police Constable Key Facts: 15,000+ vacancies/year (largest govt job). Entry Rs. 21,000–Rs. 25,000. Selection 0.8–1.5%. Physical test crucial (5km/24min, 20 pullups). Written exam moderate. Career ceiling Inspector (Rs. 65,000+). Requires discipline + fitness commitment.

What is Police Constable Role?

  • Duty: Street patrol, law enforcement, public order, traffic management, community policing
  • Shift: 8–10 hours (including nights, weekends, holidays)
  • Posted: Usually away from hometown first 5 years, then hometown possible
  • Risk: Moderate (law enforcement duties, occasional physical danger)
  • Interaction: Heavy public interaction, complaint handling, order maintenance
  • Hierarchy: Lowest rank, supervised by Head Constable/Sub-Inspector

Physical Test Breakdown: 6-Component Fitness Assessment

  • 5 km Run (OR 2.4km for female): Must complete within time limit. Aerobic capacity test. Start training 2–3 months before exam.
  • Long Jump: Minimum 3.5m (male), 2.8m (female). Explosive leg strength.
  • High Jump: Minimum 1.3m (male), 1.0m (female). Vertical power test.
  • Pull-ups: Minimum 20 (male), 8–10 (female). Upper body strength critical. Most candidates fail here.
  • Sit-ups: 40 in 2 minutes (both genders). Core strength, muscle endurance.
  • Flexibility Test: Touch-your-toes test. Range of motion, injury prevention.

Police Constable Written Exam Syllabus & Strategy

  • General Knowledge (30%): Indian history, geography, constitution, current affairs (last 6 months)
  • Reasoning (30%): Logic puzzles, pattern recognition, coding-decoding (no math-heavy reasoning)
  • English (20%): Basic comprehension, simple grammar, vocabulary
  • Quantitative (20%): Basic math (10th level), percentages, ratios (no complex calculus)
  • Exam Duration: 2–3 hours, 100–150 questions
  • Passing Score: 40–50% required
  • Prep Strategy: 4–6 months, daily 3–4 hours, solve 500+ previous year questions

Police Constable FAQ (12 Key Questions)

1. Can women apply for police constable?
YES, actively recruited. Physical standards relaxed: 2.4km run in 14 minutes (vs 5km in 24 for men). Height 152cm minimum. Women-only patrols, safety protocols strong. Encouraged to apply.
2. Will I get posted in my hometown?
Rarely for first 5 years. Police policy = transfer outside hometown to prevent bias/corruption. After 5–10 years, hometown posting possible with seniority + request.
3. How many hours per day is police duty?
8–10 hours per shift (rotating shifts: day, evening, night). Includes weekends, holidays, emergency call-outs. Practical = 50+ hours/week often.
4. What if I fail physical test first attempt?
Most states allow 2–3 attempts in same exam year. If fail all attempts, re-apply next cycle (1–2 years later).
5. Is police job safe for women?
Safety protocols in place: women-only patrols, harassment cells, separate quarters, training. Risk similar to male counterparts. Personal comfort decision important.
6. Career ceiling from constable?
Inspector (Rs. 65,000–Rs. 85,000) after 18–20 years. Some reach DCP (Rs. 1.1 lacs+) with 25+ years. Promotion exam-based + merit-based.
7. Can I apply if juvenile record exists?
Juvenile records (before 18) usually cleared after 5 years. Adult criminal record = automatic disqualification. Check state police eligibility rules before applying.
8. Timeline: Exam to actual joining?
Written → Physical → Interview → Document verification → Training center admission = 6–9 months. Training = 6 months. Total = 12–15 months from notification to uniform.
9. Is police job very stressful?
YES, high stress. Law enforcement duties, emergencies, low pay first years (Rs. 21,000), family separation early career. Work-life balance poor first 5–7 years. Consider carefully.
10. What's the pension?
50% of last salary after retirement (58–60 years). Example: Rs. 60,000 last salary = Rs. 30,000/month lifetime pension. Plus gratuity (Rs. 10–15 lacs).
11. Can I switch from police to other govt departments?
Limited moves. Police → Police SI (internal). Police → Other depts (rare). Choose ONLY if want lifelong police career.
12. Success rate in police exam?
Written pass rate = 10–15%. Physical test = 5–8% (very stringent). Overall = 0.8–1.5%. Need excellence in all stages to succeed.
