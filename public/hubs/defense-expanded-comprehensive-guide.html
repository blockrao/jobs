What is Defense Government Jobs in India?

Defense government jobs include commissioned officer roles in the Indian Army, Navy, Air Force, defense research (DRDO), and civilian defense services. These are among the most prestigious government careers with entry salaries ₹35K–₹50K, strong lifetime pensions, and international exposure. Over 2,000 positions open annually across all defense sectors.

Defense Key Facts: Entry salary ₹35K–₹50K (comparable to SSC CGL). Career ceiling ₹120K–₹150K+ (same tier as banking). Selection rate 0.5–2% (highly selective). Lifetime pension 50% of salary + family benefits. Global exposure and prestige. Fitness and discipline required.

Defense Recruitment Exams

1. CDS (Combined Defence Services) — UPSC

  • Frequency: Twice yearly (Feb and Sept)
  • Eligibility: Bachelor's degree, age 23–28 years
  • Exam: Written (2 hours, 300 MCQ) + SSB interview (5 days)
  • Selection Rate: 1% (highly competitive)
  • Services: Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard

2. AFCAT (Air Force Common Admission Test)

  • Frequency: Twice yearly (Feb and Aug)
  • Eligibility: Bachelor's degree, age 20–24 years
  • Exam: Online test (1.5 hours, 100 MCQ) + SSB interview
  • Selection Rate: 1–2% (moderately competitive)
  • Services: Air Force officer (Flying, Ground Duty, Technical)

3. NDA (National Defence Academy)

  • Frequency: Twice yearly (April and Sept)
  • Eligibility: 12th Pass, age 16.5–19 years
  • Exam: Written (2 papers) + SSB interview
  • Training: 3-year academy course (commission as 2nd Lieutenant)
  • Selection Rate: 0.5–1% (most selective)

Defense Officer Lifestyle & Benefits

  • Housing: On-campus provided (or allowance ₹15K–₹30K/month)
  • Medical: ECHS (Ex-servicemen Contributory Health Scheme) lifetime coverage
  • Education Allowance: ₹5K–₹10K/month per child
  • Leave: 110 days/year (60 casual + 30 earned + 20 privilege)
  • Pension: 50% of last-drawn salary for lifetime + family survivor benefits
  • Posting: Every 2–3 years transfer (domestic + international opportunities)

Next Steps to Defense Career

  1. Assess career choice: Officer vs civilian defense role
  2. Choose recruitment path: CDS (UPSC), AFCAT (Air Force), or SSC Defence
  3. Verify eligibility: Bachelor's degree (or 12th pass for NDA), age requirements
  4. Register for exam (exam calendars: UPSC.gov.in, AFCAT official portal)
  5. Prepare 6–12 months: General knowledge, current affairs, English, math
  6. Clear written exam → Appear for SSB interview (5-day selection process)
  7. Medical and fitness tests (strict standards)
  8. Commission as officer, join service (3–25 year career options)
