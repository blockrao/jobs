₹30K Salary Jobs: Is It Worth vs Private Sector?

Government ₹30K: Includes DA (dearness allowance), HRA (house rent), pension 50%, 120+ leave days. Actual take-home = ₹32K–₹35K. Private sector ₹30K: No DA/HRA flexibility, no pension, 25 leave days, job insecurity. **Verdict:** Government ₹30K > Private sector ₹30K by ₹5K–₹8K effective monthly value.
