What is SSC CGL?

Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) is India's premier government exam for bachelor's degree holders. Conducted annually, it offers 40,000+ permanent government positions across 15+ departments including Income Tax, Ministry of External Affairs, Railway Board, Customs, and Excise. SSC CGL recruits officers for Tier-1 (government office), Tier-2 (banks), and Tier-3 (departments) roles.

SSC CGL Key Facts: Entry salary ₹37,000/month (real data from JobOye database of 4,882 government posts). Career ceiling ₹92,000/month. Selection rate 1% (1 seat per 100 applicants). Permanent job with pension after probation.

SSC CGL Selection Process: 2-Tier System

Tier 1: Preliminary Exam (Prelims)

  • Format: 100 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes, 200 marks
  • Subjects: English (25 Q), Reasoning (25 Q), Quantitative Ability (25 Q), General Awareness (25 Q)
  • Cutoff: Top 10,000–20,000 candidates qualify (varies yearly)
  • Typical Cutoff: General 115–130/200, OBC 105–120, SC 95–110, ST 90–105
  • Marking: +2 for correct, -0.5 for wrong (negative marking)

Tier 2: Main Examination (Mains)

  • Format: 4 descriptive papers, 600 marks total, 2 days
  • Paper 1: English (200 marks) — Grammar, comprehension, essay, formal letter
  • Papers 2–4: 3 domain-specific subjects (200 marks each) — General Studies, Finance, Commerce, Statistics, etc.
  • Duration: 2 hours per paper, back-to-back
  • Qualification: Top 5,000–10,000 qualify for final selection

Interview (Optional / Minor Impact)

  • Conducted only for selected candidates (after mains)
  • 100 marks total, but weighted as 10% of final score
  • 5–10 minutes with panel, focus on personality + current affairs

SSC CGL Eligibility Criteria

  • Nationality: Indian citizen only
  • Education: Bachelor's degree from recognized university (any stream)
  • Age: 18–32 years (General category)
  • Age Relaxation:
    • OBC: Up to 35 years
    • SC/ST: Up to 37 years
    • Ex-servicemen: Up to 3 years additional
  • Physical Fitness: Varies by department (not required for all roles)
  • Language: English + one Indian language required for some departments

SSC CGL Preparation Strategy: 6–12 Month Timeline

Month 1–2: Foundation Building

Focus: Grammar basics, arithmetic fundamentals, reasoning types. Target: 70% concept clarity, 200 total questions attempted across all subjects.

Month 3–4: Subject Depth

Focus: English mastery, quantitative ability problems, reasoning patterns. Target: 80% accuracy in timed 20-minute English tests, 75% in quant.

Month 5–6: Exam-Specific Strategy

Focus: SSC previous year papers, high-frequency topics, time management. Target: 160–180/200 in mock tests, 80%+ accuracy.

Month 7–8: Final Revision & Mock Testing

Focus: Daily full-length mock tests, weak area targeting, confidence building. Target: 180–190/200, ready for actual exam.

Common Mistakes in SSC Preparation

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: Can I apply for SSC CGL after 12th pass? A: No. Bachelor's degree required. For 12th pass: Apply for SSC CHSL (entry salary ₹20K–₹30K).
  • Q: How many times can I attempt SSC CGL? A: Unlimited until age 32 (37 for SC/ST/OBC). Most candidates get 4–6 attempts.
  • Q: Is SSC CGL permanent job? A: Yes. Permanent government employment after 2-year probation. Pension ₹18K–₹25K/month post-retirement.
  • Q: When is SSC CGL notification released? A: June–July annually. Prelims in September–October. Mains in November–December.
  • Q: What is cutoff for SSC CGL? A: Varies yearly. Typical: General 115–130/200, OBC 105–120, SC 95–110, ST 90–105.
