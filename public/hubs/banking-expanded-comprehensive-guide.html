What is Banking in India?

Banking jobs in India comprise recruitment through IBPS (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection), SBI (State Bank of India), RBI (Reserve Bank of India), and private banks like ICICI, HDFC, Axis, and Kotak. These are among the most stable, secure, and well-compensated government and semi-government jobs in India. 5,000–8,000 positions open annually across all banking exams.

Banking Key Facts: Selection rate 1–2% (similar to SSC CGL). Entry salary ₹25K–₹30K (PO). Career ceiling ₹100K–₹150K+ (General Manager). Preparation timeline 3–6 months (vs 2–3 years for UPSC). Can prepare while working. Lifetime pension + job security.

IBPS Exam Structure: 3-Stage Process

Stage 1: Preliminary Exam (Prelims)

  • Format: 100 MCQ questions, 1 hour
  • Subjects: Quantitative Ability (35), Reasoning (35), English Language (30)
  • Cutoff: General 50–60/100 (varies yearly by difficulty)
  • Qualifying Candidates: Top 5,000–8,000 qualify for mains

Stage 2: Main Examination (Mains)

  • Format: 200 MCQ questions, 2 hours
  • Subjects: Quantitative Ability (50), Reasoning (50), English Language (40), General Awareness (60)
  • Cutoff: General 100–120/200 marks (varies yearly)
  • Qualifying for Interview: Top 1,000–2,000 candidates

Stage 3: Interview

  • Format: 20–30 minutes, 100 marks total
  • Focus: Resume discussion, current affairs, banking awareness, personal motivation
  • Weightage: Interview is 33% of final score (more weighted than SSC but less than UPSC)
  • Timeline: Conducted 2–3 months after mains

IBPS Exam Eligibility

  • Nationality: Indian citizen only
  • Education: Bachelor's degree minimum (any stream)
  • Age for PO: 20–30 years (Gen), 32 years (OBC), 37 years (SC/ST)
  • Age for Clerk: 18–28 years (Gen), 31 years (OBC), 36 years (SC/ST)
  • Attempts: Unlimited until age limit
  • Gap: No gap requirement; working candidates can prepare

Banking Exam Preparation: 6-Month Timeline

Month 1–2: Foundation & Skill Building

  • Complete English grammar basics (tenses, articles, prepositions)
  • Master Quantitative Ability fundamentals (number system, percentages, profit-loss)
  • Learn Logical Reasoning basics (analogy, classification, series)
  • Target: 200 questions attempted across all subjects, 70% concept clarity

Month 3–4: Subject Mastery & Mock Tests

  • Advance to harder Quant problems (time-distance, boats, ratios)
  • Advanced Reasoning (data arrangement, coding-decoding, blood relations)
  • English: Reading comprehension, sentence correction, para jumbles
  • Current Affairs: Read newspaper daily (for mains + interview)
  • Target: 1 full-length mock test every 3 days, 60% accuracy

Month 5: Exam-Specific Strategy

  • Review IBPS previous year papers (2019–2024)
  • Identify question patterns: Most repeated topics
  • Banking Awareness: Learn about RBI, current banking reforms, monetary policy
  • Full-length mocks: 3–4 per week, target 80% accuracy
  • Target score: 120–140/200 in mains mock

Month 6: Final Revision & Interview Prep

  • Daily full-length tests (simulate exam timing)
  • Weak area targeting: 2 hours daily on lowest-scoring section
  • Interview preparation: Mock interviews, resume polishing
  • Banking Awareness: Complete coverage (financial news, RBI, current rate changes)
  • Target: Mock test score 150–160/200 (ready for exam)

Banking Interview: What to Expect

  • Duration: 20–30 minutes, 100 marks total
  • Questions: Resume discussion, current affairs, bank awareness, why banking, personal interests
  • No Negative Marking: Banking interview is positive assessment (scores are additive)
  • Passing Score: 50–60/100 typical; 70+/100 considered strong
  • Weightage: Interview is 1/3 of final score (more impact than SSC but less than UPSC)

Common Mistakes in Banking Exam Prep

  • ❌ No banking awareness prep: ✅ Read banking news 1 hour/week (10% of mains + critical for interview)
  • ❌ Ignoring Current Affairs: ✅ Daily news reading (The Hindu, NDTV) mandatory for mains + interview
  • ❌ Only doing Prelims prep: ✅ Mains format is entirely different; start mains practice from Month 3
  • ❌ No speed optimization: ✅ Must solve 100 questions in 60 min with 80% accuracy
  • ❌ Interview overconfidence: ✅ Mock interviews mandatory (50+ practice rounds before real interview)
