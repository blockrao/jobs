# IIIT Delhi Recruitment Guide

Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Delhi is a premier IT education and research institute recruiting faculty, researchers, and technical professionals across computer science and related domains. With 18+ positions and continuous recruitment, IIIT Delhi offers cutting-edge technology research opportunities, competitive compensation, and influence over India's tech education.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

IIIT Delhi is renowned for IT innovation and research, offering positions in:

- **Faculty Positions:** Computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, AI/ML, data science
- **Research Scientists:** PhD holders pursuing advanced computing research
- **Technical & Administrative Roles:** System administrators, technical program managers, IT support
- **Specialized Positions:** Cybersecurity specialists, AI researchers, data science experts

IIIT Delhi positions emphasize cutting-edge technology, research innovation, and industry relevance.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD in Computer Science, IT, or related discipline
- 2–5 years postdoctoral or research experience
- Published research papers in reputable venues (minimum 4–6 papers)
- Strong programming and systems expertise
- Industry or research lab experience valued
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Scientists & Associates:**
- PhD in Computer Science/IT or M.Tech with exceptional profile
- 0–3 years research experience
- Published research (minimum 2–3 papers)
- Expertise in specialized areas (AI, cybersecurity, systems, etc.)
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Technical Specialists:**
- B.Tech in Computer Science or related field
- 3–5 years experience in IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, or systems
- Relevant technical certifications (CCNA, CISSP, etc.)
- Age: 18–35 years

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.iiitd.ac.in
- Recruitment throughout the year
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Application fee: None

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Evaluation of PhD credentials and research publications
- Quality of papers in top-tier conferences/journals
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position
- h-index and citations considered

**Step 3: Technical Interview**
- Format: Online or in-person
- Duration: 60–90 minutes
- Focus: Technical depth, research methodology, systems design
- Programming aptitude assessed

**Step 4: Research Presentation**
- 20–30 minute presentation of research area
- Emphasis on innovation and practical impact
- Q&A on technical challenges and solutions

**Step 5: Final Interview & Selection**
- Meeting with department head/director
- Discussion on research vision and teaching philosophy
- Offer letter issued with contract terms

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the typical recruitment cycle duration?**  
A: Usually 4–6 months from notification to offer, though this varies by organization and recruitment type.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple positions at the same time?**  
A: Yes, but ensure each application highlights relevant qualifications for that specific role.

**Q: What if I don't meet all the experience requirements?**  
A: You can still apply. Shortlisting criteria vary; exceptional candidates may be considered even with slightly lower experience.

**Q: How competitive is the selection process?**  
A: Very competitive. Typically 5–10 candidates are shortlisted per position, with 50+ applicants common. Quality of research/achievements matters significantly.

**Q: Are category relaxations (SC/ST/OBC) strictly applied?**  
A: Yes. Relaxations are automatically applied if you claim the category with valid certificates during document verification.

**Q: What is the interview format?**  
A: Varies by role. Technical roles may include written tests; research positions include presentations; administrative roles focus on HR and domain knowledge.

**Q: Can I negotiate the starting salary?**  
A: Limited negotiation possible for senior roles (Professor, Principal Scientist). Junior positions typically follow fixed pay commission scales.

**Q: What documents must I submit immediately after selection?**  
A: Educational certificates, experience certificates, medical fitness reports, character certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and identity proof.

## Interview Preparation Guide

**For Technical & Research Roles:**

1. **Research Your Organization:**
   - Know the organization's mission, recent achievements, and research focus areas
   - Understand the department or division you're applying for
   - Identify potential mentors or collaborators

2. **Prepare Your Story:**
   - 2-minute elevator pitch: Background, key achievements, why you're interested
   - Practice explaining your research/work in simple, engaging language
   - Prepare 3-5 examples of challenges you solved

3. **Know the Role:**
   - Review the job description carefully
   - Understand the expectations for the first 2 years
   - Prepare questions about growth, resources, and team structure

4. **Common Interview Topics:**
   - Your core expertise and how you stay current
   - Your biggest professional achievement and lessons learned
   - How you work in teams and handle disagreements
   - Your 5-year career vision
   - Why this organization, not competitors?

5. **Technical Preparation (if applicable):**
   - Review recent publications from the department
   - Brush up on fundamentals in your field
   - Prepare case studies or examples showing problem-solving ability

6. **Logistics:**
   - Schedule interview at least 48 hours in advance if possible
   - Test internet/video setup if online interview
   - Arrive 15 minutes early for in-person interviews
   - Carry extra copies of resume and certificates
   - Dress professionally (formal attire for government organizations)

7. **Questions to Ask:**
   - "What does success look like for this role in the first year?"
   - "What is the team structure and who would I be working with?"
   - "What are the main challenges in this position?"
   - "What opportunities exist for professional development?"

8. **After Interview:**
   - Send thank-you email within 24 hours
   - Mention specific discussion points to show genuine interest
   - Wait for official communication (avoid follow-up calls unless timeline was specified)
