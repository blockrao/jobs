# UPSC (Union Public Service Commission)

## Overview

UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) is India's topmost civil service recruitment body. It conducts the prestigious Civil Services Examination for recruiting officers to All India Services (IAS, IPS, IFS) and Central Services (Group A & Group B). This guide covers everything about UPSC recruitment, eligibility, salary structure, and the selection process.

## Eligibility Criteria

- **Citizenship**: Indian national
- **Age Limit**: 21-32 years for General category (relaxations available for SC/ST/OBC)
  - SC/ST: 21-37 years
  - OBC: 21-35 years
  - PwD: Relaxation of 5 years
- **Educational Qualification**: Bachelor's degree from recognized university in any stream
- **Attempts**: Maximum 6 attempts for General (9 for SC/ST, 4 for PwD)

## Salary & Benefits

UPSC selected officers receive:
- **Starting Salary**: ₹56,100 – ₹75,000+ per month (depends on service and cadre)
- **In-Hand**: ₹80,000 – ₹110,000+ after deductions
- **Benefits**:
  - Dearness Allowance (DA) - 42% of basic
  - House Rent Allowance (HRA) - ₹18,000–₹27,000
  - Hardship Allowance (for remote postings)
  - Medical & health insurance for family
  - Pension: 50% of average emoluments
  - Subsidized housing (if applicable)
  - Leave encashment & gratuity

## Selection Process

The UPSC CSE follows a rigorous three-tier selection process:

### Tier 1: Preliminary Examination
- **Type**: Objective (MCQ) format
- **Subjects**: General Studies & CSAT (Aptitude Test)
- **Duration**: 2 hours each (Objective)
- **Qualify**: Top 10,000-12,000 candidates advance
- **No negative marking in CSAT**

### Tier 2: Main Examination
- **Type**: Descriptive format (Essay + Answer writing)
- **Subjects**: 9 papers covering General Studies (4), Optional Subject (2), Essay, English, Hindi
- **Duration**: 3 hours each
- **Total Marks**: 1750
- **Qualify**: Top 2,000-2,500 candidates advance

### Tier 3: Interview
- **Type**: Personal interview by UPSC panel
- **Duration**: 40-60 minutes
- **Marks**: 275
- **Focus**: Personality, leadership, communication, subject knowledge

### Final Selection
- Combined ranking of Mains + Interview
- Medical examination and document verification
- Final list released 4-6 weeks after interviews

## Preparation Tips

1. **Strong Foundation**: Master General Studies, particularly History, Geography, Polity, Economics
2. **Current Affairs**: Daily news reading from multiple sources
3. **Consistent Study**: 8-10 hours daily for 12-18 months
4. **Mock Tests**: Take regular full-length prelims & mains tests
5. **Optional Subject**: Choose based on your interest (highly impacts Mains score)
6. **Revision**: Allot 2-3 months for intensive revision before exams
7. **Coaching** (Optional): Many crack without coaching; consider based on your learning style
8. **Mental Toughness**: Prepare for multiple attempts, rejection, and prolonged study

## Post-Selection Career

### Services & Cadres Available
- **IAS**: Indian Administrative Service (State administration)
- **IPS**: Indian Police Service (Law & order)
- **IFS**: Indian Foreign Service (Diplomacy)
- **And 20+ Central Services** (like IRTS, IDAS, IDAS, IDES, etc.)

### Career Progression
- **Year 1-3**: Junior officer with routine administrative duties
- **Year 3-10**: Senior positions, departmental responsibility
- **Year 10+**: Leadership roles, postings as DM, SP, Deputy Secretary
- **Year 30+**: Secretary-level positions, top administrative posts

### Postings & Transfer
- First posting typically after 6 months of training at LBSNAA (Mussoorie)
- Transfers every 2-3 years
- Field postings (district collector) every 3-5 years
- Opportunities for international deputation (UN, World Bank, etc.)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Attempting without proper preparation
2. Ignoring CSAT (Aptitude Test) - many candidates fall at this stage
3. Poor answer writing in Mains exam
4. Not practicing on actual exam papers & patterns
5. Choosing wrong optional subject
6. Neglecting current affairs and recent policy updates
7. Starting preparation too late (start 18-20 months before exam)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many attempts are allowed for UPSC CSE?**
A: 6 attempts for General, 9 for SC/ST, 4 for PwD candidates.

**Q: Is coaching necessary for UPSC?**
A: No, many candidates crack without coaching. Self-study with right resources is sufficient.

**Q: What is the salary after UPSC selection?**
A: IAS/IPS officers start at ₹56,100 basic + allowances, totaling ₹80,000-₹110,000+ per month.

**Q: How long is the selection process?**
A: Typically 13-16 months from notification to final results.

**Q: Can I apply for UPSC while working?**
A: Yes, many candidates prepare while working. However, full-time preparation increases success chances.
