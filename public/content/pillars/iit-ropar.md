# IIT Ropar Recruitment Guide

Indian Institute of Technology Ropar is a newer IIT established in 2009, recruiting faculty and researchers across engineering disciplines with emphasis on innovation and entrepreneurship. With 23+ positions and continuous recruitment, IIT Ropar offers a dynamic research environment, competitive compensation, and the opportunity to shape India's technical education.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

IIT Ropar is known for fostering entrepreneurial thinking and innovation, offering positions in:

- **Faculty Across Engineering Disciplines:** Computer science, electronics, mechanical, chemical engineering, etc.
- **Applied Sciences:** Physics, chemistry, mathematics
- **Research Scientists & Associates:** Postdoctoral fellows, research scientists
- **Technical & Administrative Staff:** Lab managers, IT support, institutional planning

IIT Ropar positions emphasize research innovation, startup mentality, and direct student impact.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD in relevant engineering or science discipline
- 2–5 years postdoctoral or research experience
- Published research in quality journals/conferences (minimum 3–5 papers)
- Innovation potential and entrepreneurial thinking valued
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Scientists & Associates:**
- PhD in relevant discipline
- 0–3 years research experience
- Published research (minimum 2–3 papers)
- Entrepreneurial or innovation interest
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

## Salary & Benefits Structure

**Faculty:**
- **Assistant Professor:** ₹70K–₹105K/month
- **Associate Professor:** ₹105K–₹155K/month
- HRA: 30–35%, DA: 52%
- Research grants: ₹8–₹20 lakh annually
- Startup support allowance: ₹1K–₹3K/month
- Innovation bonus: Up to 2 months annually

**Research Scientists:**
- ₹50K–₹85K/month
- Research contingency: ₹2K–₹5K/month
- Startup mentoring honorarium available

**Benefits (All Positions):**
- Government medical insurance (CGHS)
- Non-contributory pension after 10 years
- Campus housing or housing allowance
- Professional development fund
- Startup incubation support
- Conference travel allowance
- Subsidized institutional facilities
- Children's education allowance

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.iitrpr.ac.in
- Recruitment throughout the year
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: None

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Evaluation of publications and research innovation
- Entrepreneurship potential assessed
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position

**Step 3: Research Presentation**
- 20–30 minute presentation of research
- Emphasis on innovation and future vision
- Q&A on research methodology

**Step 4: Technical Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior faculty, department head
- Topics: Research depth, innovation potential, teaching philosophy
- Discussion of entrepreneurial interests

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter issued
- Contract typically 3 years

## How to Apply for IIT Ropar Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.iitrpr.ac.in/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email registration
3. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - PhD degree certificate
   - Curriculum Vitae with publication list
   - Research statement focusing on innovation (600 words)
   - List of all publications with DOIs
   - Experience certificates
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
   - Optional: Innovation/startup interests documentation
5. **Submit:** Online submission
6. **Fee:** None

## Selection Process

**Research Innovation & Entrepreneurship Focus:**

**Innovation Potential Assessment:**
- Uniqueness of research contributions
- Patents or technology innovations
- Startup or entrepreneurial thinking
- Ability to create new research directions
- Technology transfer potential

**Interview Topics:**
- Your research innovations and unique contributions
- Entrepreneurial thinking and startup interests
- How you'd foster innovation in student projects
- Future research vision with practical impact
- Experience with technology development
- Mentoring capability for student startups
- Interdisciplinary collaboration potential

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most innovative research contribution"
- "How would you foster entrepreneurial thinking in your lab?"
- "Tell us about patents or technology innovations you've developed"
- "What's your vision for research-based startups?"
- "How do you balance academic rigor with practical innovation?"
- "Describe your experience mentoring student entrepreneurs"
- "How would you contribute to IIT Ropar's innovation ecosystem?"

## Key Points for Success

- **Research Innovation:** Demonstrated innovative contributions to field
- **Entrepreneurial Mindset:** Interest in technology translation and startups
- **Student Impact:** Ability to mentor students toward innovation
- **Technology Development:** Experience with patents or technology products
- **Collaboration:** Interdisciplinary thinking and partnership capability
