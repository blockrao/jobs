# ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation)

## Overview

ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation) is a major recruiting organization in the Indian space sector. This guide covers everything you need to know about applying, eligibility criteria, salary expectations, and the selection process.

## Eligibility

- Citizenship: Indian national
- Educational qualification: As specified in recruitment notification
- Age limit: Generally 18-40 years (varies by position and post)
- Physical standards: As per recruitment requirements

## Salary & Benefits

Based on recent ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation) recruitments:
- **Starting salary**: ₹25,000 – ₹60,000 per month
- **Benefits**: 
  - Dearness Allowance (DA)
  - House Rent Allowance (HRA)
  - Medical & health insurance
  - Pension/Gratuity (as per rules)
  - Performance bonuses
  - Leave encashment

## Application Process

1. Check official website for recruitment notification
2. Register on the application portal
3. Fill application form with required details
4. Upload scanned documents (certificates, ID)
5. Pay application fee online
6. Submit application and take confirmation

## Selection Process

### Written Examination
- Objective type multiple choice questions
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Syllabus: General Knowledge, English, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude
- Negative marking: 0.25-0.5 marks per wrong answer

### Interview
- Personal interview with selection committee
- Documents verification
- Medical examination

### Final Selection
- Merit list preparation
- Appointment letter issuance
- Joining formalities

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the minimum educational qualification?**
A: Varies by position. Check the specific recruitment notification.

**Q: Can I apply from outside India?**
A: Generally, Indian citizenship is mandatory. Non-resident Indians may have specific provisions.

**Q: How many attempts are allowed?**
A: Depends on age limit and recruitment rules. Check notification.

**Q: Is there a negative marking in exams?**
A: Yes, typically 0.25-0.5 marks deducted for wrong answers.

**Q: When are results announced?**
A: Usually 4-6 weeks after the exam. Check official website for updates.
