# IIT Roorkee Recruitment Guide

Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee), India's second-oldest technical institute, recruits faculty and researchers across all engineering disciplines and applied sciences. With 27+ positions and continuous recruitment, IIT Roorkee offers world-class research infrastructure, competitive compensation, and the opportunity to mentor future engineering leaders.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

IIT Roorkee is renowned for excellence in civil, mechanical, electrical, chemical engineering and applied sciences. Offering positions in:

- **Faculty Across All Engineering Disciplines:** Civil, mechanical, electrical, chemical, computer science, etc.
- **Applied Sciences:** Physics, chemistry, mathematics, humanities
- **Research Scientists:** Postdoctoral fellows, research associates
- **Technical & Administrative Roles:** Laboratory managers, institutional support

IIT Roorkee positions provide access to premier research facilities, a distinguished faculty community, and influence over engineering education quality.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD in relevant engineering or science discipline
- 3–8 years postdoctoral or research experience (depending on rank)
- Published research in reputable journals and conferences (minimum 5–10 papers)
- Strong publication record and research citations
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Associates & Scientists:**
- PhD in relevant discipline
- 0–3 years experience in research institutions
- Published research (minimum 2–4 papers)
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Important:** IIT Roorkee prioritizes research quality, publication record, and demonstrated innovation potential.

## Salary & Benefits Structure

**Faculty:**
- **Assistant Professor:** ₹75K–₹110K/month
- **Associate Professor:** ₹110K–₹160K/month
- **Professor:** ₹150K–₹230K+/month
- HRA: 30–35%, DA: 52%
- Research grants: ₹10–₹25 lakh annually (assistant prof)
- Senior faculty research support: Up to ₹50 lakh
- Performance bonus: Up to 3–4 months annually

**Research Scientists:**
- ₹50K–₹85K/month
- Research contingency allowance: ₹2K–₹5K/month

**Benefits (All Positions):**
- Government medical insurance (CGHS)
- Non-contributory pension after 10 years
- Campus housing or housing allowance
- Professional development fund: ₹50K–₹3 lakh annually
- Conference travel support
- Subsidized institutional facilities
- Children's education allowance
- Gratuity benefits for senior faculty
- Provident fund

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.iitr.ac.in
- Recruitment throughout the year
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: None

**Step 2: Shortlisting Based on Research**
- PhD credentials and publication quality essential
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position
- h-index and citation impact considered

**Step 3: Written Test (Some Departments)**
- Assessment of specialized technical knowledge
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Focus: Domain expertise and research methodology

**Step 4: Research Presentation & Interview**
- 20–30 minute research presentation
- 60–90 minute technical interview with senior faculty
- Discussion of research vision for IIT Roorkee

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter issued
- Contract typically 3 years

## How to Apply for IIT Roorkee Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.iitr.ac.in/recruitment or department website
2. **Create Account:** Email-based registration
3. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - PhD degree certificate
   - Curriculum Vitae with publication list
   - Research statement (600–800 words)
   - List of all publications with DOIs/links
   - Experience certificates from previous institutions
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
   - Optional: Recommendation letters (2–3)
5. **Submit:** Online portal submission
6. **Fee:** None

## Selection Process

**Research Presentation & Interview Focus:**

**Publication Quality & Impact:**
- Number of peer-reviewed publications
- Quality of venues (top-tier conferences/journals)
- Citation count and h-index
- Uniqueness and innovation in your research

**Interview Topics:**
- Your most significant research contribution
- Future research vision and direction
- Ability to supervise PhD students
- Teaching philosophy and course design
- Interdisciplinary research potential
- Why IIT Roorkee?
- Experience with research grants and funding
- Collaboration opportunities within institution

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most impactful research publication"
- "How would you develop a research group at IIT Roorkee?"
- "What's your teaching philosophy?"
- "Tell us about your experience with research funding"
- "How do you contribute to interdisciplinary research?"
- "What's your vision for your field in next 5 years?"
- "How would you mentor doctoral students?"

**Document Verification:**
- PhD degree verification
- Publication quality assessment in academic databases
- Employment history verification
- Research grant verification
- Medical fitness clearance
- Character verification
