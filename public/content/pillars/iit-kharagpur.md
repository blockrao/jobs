# IIT Kharagpur Recruitment Guide

Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur is one of India's oldest and most prestigious engineering institutes, recruiting faculty across all engineering disciplines, applied sciences, and management. With 33+ positions and continuous recruitment cycles, IIT Kharagpur offers world-class research facilities, competitive compensation, and the opportunity to mentor future leaders.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

IIT Kharagpur stands as one of Asia's premier engineering institutions, known for:

- **Faculty Excellence:** Teaching and research in mechanical, civil, electrical, chemical, metallurgical, mining, aerospace, and electronics engineering
- **Applied Sciences:** Physics, chemistry, mathematics, humanities
- **Management & Business:** MBA and professional studies
- **Cutting-Edge Research:** State-of-the-art laboratories and research centers
- **Global Recognition:** Strong international collaborations and publications

Faculty positions at IIT Kharagpur offer access to premium research infrastructure, distinguished colleagues, and influence over India's engineering education.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD in relevant engineering or science discipline
- 2–8 years postdoctoral/research experience (depending on career stage)
- Published research in peer-reviewed journals and conferences (minimum 5–10 papers)
- Strong publication record and citation impact
- Demonstrated research potential and innovation
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Associates & Postdoctoral Fellows:**
- PhD in relevant discipline or exceptional M.Tech profile
- 0–3 years experience
- Published research (minimum 2–4 papers)
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Important Requirements:**
- GATE or equivalent qualification for certain technical roles
- Industry experience valuable for applied engineering positions

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.iitkgp.ac.in
- Recruitment throughout the year (no fixed windows)
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: None (free for all candidates)

**Step 2: Shortlisting Based on Publications**
- Evaluation of research publications, quality, and impact
- Citation analysis and h-index consideration
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position
- Candidates notified via email

**Step 3: Written Test (Optional)**
- Some departments conduct written test on research aptitude
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Focus: Specialized knowledge in the field

**Step 4: Seminar & Interview**
- 20–30 minute research presentation
- 60–90 minute technical interview with senior faculty
- Panel evaluation on research depth and teaching ability
- Discussion of research vision at IIT Kharagpur

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer**
- Merit list compiled from seminar and interview scores
- Offer letter issued
- Contract period: Usually 3 years initially (renewable)

## How to Apply for IIT Kharagpur Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/recruitment or department websites
2. **Create Account:** Email-based registration
3. **Download Application Form:** Usually available as PDF
4. **Fill Details:** Personal, educational, professional information
5. **Upload Documents:**
   - PhD degree certificate
   - Complete Curriculum Vitae with publication list
   - Research statement (600–800 words on research area and vision)
   - List of all publications with links/DOIs
   - Experience certificates from previous institutions
   - Aadhaar/passport copy and recent photograph
   - Optional: Letters of recommendation (2–3 from senior researchers)
6. **Submit:** Online submission through portal
7. **Confirmation:** Receive email confirmation with application ID

**Application Fee:** None

## Selection Process

**Research Presentation & Interview Focus:**

**For Faculty Roles:**

**Written Test (if conducted):**
- Specialized technical knowledge in relevant discipline
- Research methodology and statistics
- Current trends in your field
- Application of research to real-world problems
- Pass mark: Typically 50%+

**Research Seminar:**
- Clarity and technical depth of your PhD research
- Quality and relevance of publications
- Innovation and unique contributions
- Future research direction and plans
- Fit with IIT Kharagpur's research priorities

**Interview Topics:**
- Detailed discussion of your most significant research contributions
- Publications, citations, and research impact
- Experience supervising students and postdoctoral fellows
- Teaching philosophy and course development approach
- Interdisciplinary research potential
- Collaboration with industry and other institutions
- Why IIT Kharagpur? Your vision for the institute
- Ability to mentor and develop junior researchers

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most impactful research publication and why"
- "How would you develop a research group at IIT Kharagpur?"
- "What's your approach to balancing research and teaching?"
- "Tell us about a challenging research problem you've solved"
- "How do you contribute to interdisciplinary research initiatives?"
- "Describe your experience with research grants and funding"
- "What innovations do you envision in your field for next 5 years?"

**Document Verification:**
- PhD degree verification with issuing institution
- Publication verification in academic databases
- Employment history cross-check
- Research grant and funding verification
- Medical fitness clearance
- Character verification

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the typical recruitment cycle duration?**  
A: Usually 4–6 months from notification to offer, though this varies by organization and recruitment type.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple positions at the same time?**  
A: Yes, but ensure each application highlights relevant qualifications for that specific role.

**Q: What if I don't meet all the experience requirements?**  
A: You can still apply. Shortlisting criteria vary; exceptional candidates may be considered even with slightly lower experience.

**Q: How competitive is the selection process?**  
A: Very competitive. Typically 5–10 candidates are shortlisted per position, with 50+ applicants common. Quality of research/achievements matters significantly.

**Q: Are category relaxations (SC/ST/OBC) strictly applied?**  
A: Yes. Relaxations are automatically applied if you claim the category with valid certificates during document verification.

**Q: What is the interview format?**  
A: Varies by role. Technical roles may include written tests; research positions include presentations; administrative roles focus on HR and domain knowledge.

**Q: Can I negotiate the starting salary?**  
A: Limited negotiation possible for senior roles (Professor, Principal Scientist). Junior positions typically follow fixed pay commission scales.

**Q: What documents must I submit immediately after selection?**  
A: Educational certificates, experience certificates, medical fitness reports, character certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and identity proof.

## Interview Preparation Guide

**For Technical & Research Roles:**

1. **Research Your Organization:**
   - Know the organization's mission, recent achievements, and research focus areas
   - Understand the department or division you're applying for
   - Identify potential mentors or collaborators

2. **Prepare Your Story:**
   - 2-minute elevator pitch: Background, key achievements, why you're interested
   - Practice explaining your research/work in simple, engaging language
   - Prepare 3-5 examples of challenges you solved

3. **Know the Role:**
   - Review the job description carefully
   - Understand the expectations for the first 2 years
   - Prepare questions about growth, resources, and team structure

4. **Common Interview Topics:**
   - Your core expertise and how you stay current
   - Your biggest professional achievement and lessons learned
   - How you work in teams and handle disagreements
   - Your 5-year career vision
   - Why this organization, not competitors?

5. **Technical Preparation (if applicable):**
   - Review recent publications from the department
   - Brush up on fundamentals in your field
   - Prepare case studies or examples showing problem-solving ability

6. **Logistics:**
   - Schedule interview at least 48 hours in advance if possible
   - Test internet/video setup if online interview
   - Arrive 15 minutes early for in-person interviews
   - Carry extra copies of resume and certificates
   - Dress professionally (formal attire for government organizations)

7. **Questions to Ask:**
   - "What does success look like for this role in the first year?"
   - "What is the team structure and who would I be working with?"
   - "What are the main challenges in this position?"
   - "What opportunities exist for professional development?"

8. **After Interview:**
   - Send thank-you email within 24 hours
   - Mention specific discussion points to show genuine interest
   - Wait for official communication (avoid follow-up calls unless timeline was specified)
