# SBI (State Bank of India)

## Overview

State Bank of India (SBI) is India's largest bank by assets and a major employer in the banking sector. SBI conducts regular recruitment for various positions including Probationary Officers (POs), Clerical Staff, Specialist Officers, and management roles. This guide covers eligibility, salary structure, selection process, and career opportunities at SBI.

## Organization Profile

- **Established**: 1955 (as central bank), 2008 (as SBI in current form)
- **Status**: Public Sector Undertaking (PSU), Listed on NSE/BSE
- **Workforce**: 300,000+ employees across 24,000+ branches
- **Headquarters**: Mumbai, Maharashtra
- **Global Presence**: 190+ countries with international presence

## Eligibility Criteria

### Probationary Officer (PO) - Entry Level
- **Graduation**: Bachelor's degree in any stream from recognized university
- **Age**: 20-30 years (OBC: 20-33 years, SC/ST: 20-35 years)
- **Citizenship**: Indian national
- **Attempts**: Typically 4 attempts for all (PwD: 10 attempts)

### Clerk (Junior Associate)
- **12th Pass** from recognized board
- **Age**: 18-28 years (OBC: 18-31 years, SC/ST: 18-33 years)
- **Citizenship**: Indian national

### Specialist Officers
- **Subject-Specific Degree**: B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com (IT, Finance, etc.)
- **Experience**: 0-2 years (varies by role)
- **Age**: Usually 20-40 years (role dependent)

### Management Positions
- **MBA/PG Degree** in relevant field
- **Experience**: 5-15+ years (role dependent)
- **Age**: 30-55 years (depends on seniority level)

## Selection Process

### For Probationary Officer (PO)

**Stage 1: Preliminary Examination**
- Objective (MCQ) format
- Duration: 1 hour
- Total Marks: 100
- Subjects: English (30), Quantitative Aptitude (35), Reasoning (35)
- Qualifying Mark: 40-45%
- Candidates Qualify: Top 10,000-12,000

**Stage 2: Main Examination**
- Descriptive format (Essay + Letter writing)
- Duration: 3 hours
- Total Marks: 200
- Subjects: English (50), Data Analysis (50), Reasoning (50), General Awareness (50)
- Qualifying Mark: 40-45% (sectional cutoffs apply)
- Candidates Qualify: Top 1,500-2,000

**Stage 3: Interview**
- Personal interview by interview panel
- Duration: 20-30 minutes
- Total Marks: 100
- Focus: Personality, communication, domain knowledge, motivation
- Candidates Selected: Final 600-800 (based on vacancies)

### For Clerk/Junior Associate

**Single Level Examination:**
- Objective (MCQ) format
- Duration: 1 hour
- Total Marks: 100
- Subjects: English (30), Quantitative Aptitude (35), Reasoning (35)
- Qualifying Mark: 40-45% (sectional & overall)
- Selection: Merit-based

### For Specialist Officers
- Written Exam (Technical + General Awareness)
- Interview
- Document verification
- Medical examination

## Preparation Tips

1. **Numerical Ability**: Practice speed + accuracy (BODMAS, percentages, ratios)
2. **English**: Grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension
3. **Reasoning**: Puzzles, logical deduction, pattern recognition
4. **General Awareness**: Banking, current affairs, economy news
5. **Speed Practice**: Mock tests to improve time management
6. **Consistency**: 4-6 hours daily study for 3-4 months
7. **Current Affairs**: Daily GK reading from reliable sources
8. **Interview Prep**: Practice HR + technical questions specific to banking

## Common Mistakes in SBI Recruitment

1. Over-confidence after clearing Prelims
2. Ignoring time management in Main exam
3. Poor English answer writing (in Main exam essay section)
4. Not focusing on accuracy (negative marking impacts score)
5. Weak interview preparation despite clearing written exams
6. Ignoring sectional cutoffs in Main exam
7. Not updating current affairs knowledge
