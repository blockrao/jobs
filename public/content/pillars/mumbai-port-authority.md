## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Port Engineers:**
- B.Tech/B.E in relevant discipline (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Marine)
- 3–8 years experience in port/maritime/infrastructure projects
- Knowledge of port operations and standards
- GATE qualification beneficial
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Administrative Officers:**
- B.A/B.Com/B.Sc or MBA
- 3–8 years experience in port operations, customs, or government
- Knowledge of port procedures and maritime regulations
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations available)

**Traffic & Cargo Specialists:**
- Bachelor's degree (any discipline)
- 2–5 years experience in cargo/maritime operations
- Knowledge of port logistics
- Age: 18–35 years

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.mumbaiport.gov.in
- Recruitment: 2–3 cycles yearly
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: ₹200–₹500 (Free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Technical Test**
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Topics: Port operations, maritime regulations, technical knowledge
- For engineers: Civil/mechanical/electrical fundamentals
- Pass mark: 40–50%

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior port engineers, administrative officers
- Topics: Port operations experience, maritime knowledge, technical expertise
- Evaluation: Technical knowledge (40%), experience (30%), communication (20%), port fit (10%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Medical Fitness**
- Experience certificates verified
- Educational qualifications cross-checked
- Port operations knowledge assessment
- Medical fitness examination
- Background verification
- Offer letter issued
