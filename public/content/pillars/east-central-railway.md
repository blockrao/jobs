# East Central Railway Recruitment Guide

East Central Railway operates one of India's busiest railway networks, recruiting technical engineers, station masters, operations staff, and administrative professionals. With 26+ positions and continuous recruitment cycles, ECR offers railway infrastructure expertise, government benefits, and direct impact on India's transportation network.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

## Overview

East Central Railway covers critical rail corridors through Eastern and Central India, offering positions in:

- **Technical Engineers:** Civil, mechanical, electrical, signals and telecommunications
- **Station Masters & Operations:** Station management, passenger services, freight operations
- **Traffic & Goods Staff:** Freight handling, passenger ticketing, revenue management
- **Administrative Roles:** Personnel, accounts, planning, institutional management
- **Safety & Inspection:** Railway safety enforcement, infrastructure inspection

ECR positions provide direct infrastructure management responsibility, government pension security, and impact on regional economic development.

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Technical Engineers:**
- B.Tech/B.E in relevant discipline (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, E&TC)
- 3–8 years experience in railway/infrastructure operations
- Knowledge of railway systems and maintenance standards
- GATE qualification beneficial
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Station Masters & Operations:**
- B.A/B.Sc/B.Tech (any discipline)
- 3–8 years experience in railway operations
- Knowledge of railway rules, freight, and passenger operations
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations available)

**Goods & Traffic Staff:**
- 10+2 (High School) with relevant training certification
- 2–5 years experience in railway freight/passenger operations
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.ecr.indianrailways.gov.in
- Recruitment: 2–3 cycles yearly
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: ₹200–₹500 (Free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Technical Test**
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Topics: Railway operations, infrastructure systems, safety standards
- For engineers: Technical knowledge specific to discipline
- Pass mark: 40–50%

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior railway engineers, station masters
- Topics: Experience, technical expertise, operations knowledge
- Evaluation: Technical knowledge (50%), experience (30%), communication (20%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Medical Fitness**
- Experience certificates verified
- Technical qualifications cross-checked
- Railway operations knowledge assessment
- Medical fitness examination (standard)
- Background verification
- Offer letter issued
