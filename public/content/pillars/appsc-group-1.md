# APPSC Group I Recruitment Guide: State Administration

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) conducts prestigious state-level exams for civil service roles. With 40+ open positions and 18 recruitment cycles, APPSC Group I is your pathway to top administrative roles in Andhra Pradesh state government. Competitive salaries, prestigious posting, and lifetime career growth.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**APPSC Group I (IAS-equivalent roles):**
- Bachelor's degree (any discipline)
- Age: 21–32 years (relaxations: SC/ST/OBC/PwD)
- Indian citizen
- No upper-age limit for SC/ST

**Common Positions:**
- Deputy Collector (IAS-equivalent)
- Superintendent of Police (state)
- Executive Officer (revenue)
- District Administration roles

---

## APPSC Salary & Benefits Structure

**Deputy Collector/IAS-Equivalent:**
- Entry salary: ₹75K–₹100K/month
- Senior positions: ₹150K–₹200K+/month
- HRA: 40–45% (tier-2 city allowances)
- DA: 53% (subject to revision)
- Special allowances: ₹5K–₹20K (posting-dependent)
- Pension: Non-contributory (50 lakh+ lifetime)
- Gratuity: Upto ₹100 lakh (senior positions)

**Benefits:**
- Government housing/HRA
- CGHS medical insurance
- Child education allowance: ₹1,000–₹2,000/month per child
- Leave travel concession: Every 2 years
- Vehicle advance for official cars

## APPSC Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step cycle (typically 6–9 months):**

**Step 1: Notification (Ongoing)**
- Website: https://www.psc.ap.gov.in
- Multiple notification rounds per year
- Deadline: 30 days from publication

**Step 2: Written Exams (Prelims + Mains)**
- Prelim: 200 MCQs, 2 hours, 600 marks
- Subjects: Current affairs, AP geography, constitution
- Mains: 5 papers, 30 marks each, 3 hours per paper
- Shortlist: Top 5–10 per position advance to mains

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: IAS officers, senior officials, AP expert
- Evaluation: Knowledge (40%), personality (40%), fit (20%)

**Step 4: Final Selection + Posting**
- Merit list finalized
- Posting order issued
- Typically immediate or within 2 months

## How to Apply for APPSC Positions

1. **Visit:** https://www.psc.ap.gov.in
2. **Register:** Create account with email, phone
3. **Select Exam:** Choose Group I exam
4. **Fill Details:** Personal, educational, professional info
5. **Upload:** Degree certificate, photo, signature
6. **Fee:** ₹500 (General/OBC), Free (SC/ST/PwD)
7. **Submit:** Confirmation email with reference number

## APPSC Selection Process

**Written Exam Focus:**
- General knowledge (Current affairs, AP state)
- History, culture, geography of AP
- Constitution, governance structure
- Quantitative aptitude, English

**Interview Topics:**
- Administrative experience and vision
- Knowledge of AP state policies
- Leadership and decision-making
- Handling public grievances
- Long-term career goals in civil service
