## Overview

BPSC conducts competitive examinations for civil service positions throughout Bihar state government. The commission recruits for:

- **Teaching roles:** Thousands of teacher positions across primary and secondary schools
- **Administrative positions:** Revenue officials, excise inspectors, commercial tax officers
- **Police & Security:** District-level administrative police roles
- **Health & Social Services:** Government hospital staff, health coordinators

BPSC positions offer job security, government pension, and the opportunity to serve Bihar's development across key sectors.

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**General Eligibility:**
- Bachelor's degree from recognized university (any discipline)
- Age: 21–32 years (General)
- Age: 21–37 years (SC/ST candidates)
- Age: 21–35 years (OBC candidates)
- Relaxations available for PwD and other reserved categories
- Indian citizen with Bihar residence preference
- No criminal record or departmental action

**Common Position-Specific Requirements:**

**Teacher Positions (Most Common):**
- Bachelor's degree + B.Ed or equivalent teaching qualification
- State teaching eligibility certificate (STET)
- Age: 21–40 years (with relaxations)

**Revenue/Administrative Roles:**
- Bachelor's degree in any discipline
- Administrative aptitude and decision-making ability

**Excise Inspector:**
- Bachelor's degree (Law or Commerce preferred)
- Basic knowledge of tax and commercial regulations

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**3-Step Cycle (Typically 3–5 months):**

**Step 1: Written Examination (Weeks 1–6)**
- Website: https://www.bpsc.bih.nic.in
- Online application with photo and signature upload
- Application deadline: 30 days from notification
- Application fee: ₹200–₹500 depending on position (waived for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Exam (Usually 4–6 weeks after applications close)**
- Format: 200 MCQs, 2 hours total, 400 marks
- Topics: General knowledge, Bihar state affairs, constitution, current events
- Pass mark: Typically 40–50% overall
- Shortlist: Top 5–10 scorers per position advance to interview

**Step 3: Interview (Weeks 4–6 after exam results)**
- Panel: BPSC officials + subject matter experts
- Duration: 30–45 minutes
- Evaluation: General knowledge (40%), Bihar policy knowledge (30%), communication (20%), personality (10%)

**Step 4: Final Selection & Posting (Weeks 8–12)**
- Merit list released combining written exam (80%) and interview (20%)
- Posting orders issued for selected candidates
- Typically immediate posting or within 2 months
- Medical fitness clearance required

## How to Apply for BPSC Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.bpsc.bih.nic.in/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email and mobile number registration
3. **Select Exam:** Choose your target position from available notifications
4. **Fill Application Form:** Personal details, education, residence information
5. **Upload Documents:**
   - Recent passport-size photograph (5 x 5 cm)
   - Signature scan (black ink on white paper)
   - Bachelor's degree certificate copy
   - Birth certificate or school leaving certificate
   - Caste certificate (if applicable)
   - Character certificate from school/college
   - Aadhaar or voter ID copy
6. **Pay Application Fee:** ₹200–₹500 online (Debit/Credit card, Net Banking)
   - SC/ST/PwD candidates: No fee
7. **Submit:** Final submission generates application ID
8. **Print Confirmation:** Keep the application ID and receipt

**Important Dates:**
- Application deadline: 30 days from notification
- Admit card release: 1 week before exam
- Written exam: Usually 6–8 weeks after deadline
- Interview: 4–6 weeks after exam results
- Final posting: 12–16 weeks total from notification

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- Incorrect educational qualification entries (verification is strict)
- Missing or unclear upload documents
- Applying for wrong category (SC/ST/OBC/General)
- Late fee payment (deadline is firm)
- Typos in name or father's name (must match documents exactly)
- Submitting non-certified photocopies
- Invalid email/phone (blocks admit card delivery)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the typical recruitment cycle duration?**  
A: Usually 4–6 months from notification to offer, though this varies by organization and recruitment type.

**Q: Can I apply for multiple positions at the same time?**  
A: Yes, but ensure each application highlights relevant qualifications for that specific role.

**Q: What if I don't meet all the experience requirements?**  
A: You can still apply. Shortlisting criteria vary; exceptional candidates may be considered even with slightly lower experience.

**Q: How competitive is the selection process?**  
A: Very competitive. Typically 5–10 candidates are shortlisted per position, with 50+ applicants common. Quality of research/achievements matters significantly.

**Q: Are category relaxations (SC/ST/OBC) strictly applied?**  
A: Yes. Relaxations are automatically applied if you claim the category with valid certificates during document verification.

**Q: What is the interview format?**  
A: Varies by role. Technical roles may include written tests; research positions include presentations; administrative roles focus on HR and domain knowledge.

**Q: Can I negotiate the starting salary?**  
A: Limited negotiation possible for senior roles (Professor, Principal Scientist). Junior positions typically follow fixed pay commission scales.

**Q: What documents must I submit immediately after selection?**  
A: Educational certificates, experience certificates, medical fitness reports, character certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and identity proof.

## Interview Preparation Guide

**For Technical & Research Roles:**

1. **Research Your Organization:**

   - Understand the department or division you're applying for
   - Identify potential mentors or collaborators

2. **Prepare Your Story:**
   - 2-minute elevator pitch: Background, key achievements, why you're interested
   - Practice explaining your research/work in simple, engaging language
   - Prepare 3-5 examples of challenges you solved

3. **Know the Role:**
   - Review the job description carefully
   - Understand the expectations for the first 2 years
   - Prepare questions about growth, resources, and team structure

4. **Common Interview Topics:**
   - Your core expertise and how you stay current
   - Your biggest professional achievement and lessons learned
   - How you work in teams and handle disagreements
   - Your 5-year career vision
   - Why this organization, not competitors?

5. **Technical Preparation (if applicable):**
   - Review recent publications from the department
   - Brush up on fundamentals in your field
   - Prepare case studies or examples showing problem-solving ability

6. **Logistics:**
   - Schedule interview at least 48 hours in advance if possible
   - Test internet/video setup if online interview
   - Arrive 15 minutes early for in-person interviews
   - Carry extra copies of resume and certificates
   - Dress professionally (formal attire for government organizations)

7. **Questions to Ask:**
   - "What does success look like for this role in the first year?"
   - "What is the team structure and who would I be working with?"

   - "What opportunities exist for professional development?"
