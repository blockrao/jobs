# ANGRAU Recruitment Guide

Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University (ANGRAU) in Andhra Pradesh is a premier agricultural research and education institution, recruiting agricultural scientists, veterinary professionals, and technical staff. With 33+ positions and continuous recruitment cycles, ANGRAU offers research-focused careers contributing to Andhra Pradesh's agricultural advancement.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

ANGRAU is one of India's oldest and most respected agricultural universities, offering:

- **Agricultural Scientists:** Crop improvement, soil science, entomology, agricultural extension
- **Veterinary Professionals:** Animal husbandry, veterinary medicine, dairy science
- **Technical & Research Staff:** Field researchers, laboratory technicians, extension coordinators
- **Administrative Roles:** Accounts, management, institutional planning

ANGRAU positions offer direct impact on farmer prosperity, government job security, and opportunities to conduct field-based agricultural research.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Agricultural Scientists:**
- PhD in relevant field (Agronomy, Horticulture, Plant Pathology, Soil Science, Agricultural Economics)
- 2+ years postdoctoral research experience
- Published research (minimum 3–5 papers in agricultural journals)
- Field research experience preferred
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Veterinary Professionals:**
- BVSc & AH (Bachelor of Veterinary Science & Animal Husbandry) + MVSc
- 2+ years experience in veterinary research or extension
- Livestock/dairy management experience preferred
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research & Technical Staff:**
- M.Sc in agriculture, veterinary science, or related field
- 1–3 years experience in research institutions
- Age: 21–40 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)
- Basic computer proficiency

- **Entry Level:** ₹65K–₹95K/month

- DA: 52%, HRA: 30%
- Research grants: ₹8–₹18 lakh annually
- Publication allowance: ₹400–₹800 per paper
- Field work allowance: ₹3K–₹8K (where applicable)

**Benefits (All Positions):**

- Conference and workshop travel support
- Field work and research contingency allowance
- Subsidized canteen and recreational facilities

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.angrau.ac.in
- Recruitment cycles: Multiple throughout the year
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Application fee: None

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Criteria: PhD + research publications in agriculture
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position
- Publication quality and field relevance evaluated

**Step 3: Research Presentation**
- 30-minute presentation of PhD research
- Focus on agricultural relevance and farmer impact
- Q&A by senior scientists on methodology

**Step 4: Interview (45–60 minutes)**
- Panel: ANGRAU senior scientists, extension coordinators
- Topics: Field experience, farmer engagement, research vision
- Evaluation: Research depth, communication, field knowledge

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter issued
- Posting location assigned
- Contract typically 3–5 years

6. **Fee:** None (free for all candidates)

## Selection Process

**Research Presentation & Interview Focus:**

**Agricultural Relevance:**
- How your research benefits farmers
- Field trial experience and outcomes
- Extension and farmer engagement work
- Soil health and sustainability focus
- Crop productivity improvements

**Interview Topics:**
- Your most impactful agricultural research
- Experience working with farming communities
- Understanding of Andhra Pradesh agricultural challenges
- Extension approach and farmer education
- Ability to supervise and mentor researchers
- Vision for agricultural advancement
- Interdisciplinary collaboration (veterinary, economics, etc.)

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe a research project that directly benefited farmers"
- "How would you engage with farming communities in your research?"
- "What's the focus of your agricultural research?"
- "Tell us about your field trial management experience"
- "How do you bridge the gap between research and farmer practice?"
- "What's your approach to extension work?"
- "How would you supervise students in field research?"

**Key Evaluation Criteria:**
- Research publications in agricultural journals
- Field experience and practical application
- Farmer engagement record
- Teaching and mentoring ability
- Sustainability focus in research
