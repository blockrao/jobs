# RITES Recruitment Guide

Rail India Technical and Economic Service (RITES) is India's leading railway consultancy and engineering service, recruiting technical engineers, economists, and senior administrative officers. With 31+ positions and multiple recruitment cycles, RITES offers specialized railway expertise, competitive compensation, and the opportunity to shape India's rail infrastructure development.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

## Overview

RITES conducts recruitment for specialized positions supporting India's railway modernization:

- **Railway Engineers:** Civil, electrical, mechanical, signals and telecommunications engineering
- **Railway Economists:** Transportation economics, project planning and analysis
- **Technical Officers:** Railway infrastructure, systems, and operations
- **Senior Administrative Officers:** Project management, institutional planning
- **Support & Specialist Roles:** IT, HR, finance, quality management

RITES positions offer direct influence on national railway projects, government benefits, and cutting-edge infrastructure development work.

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Railway Engineers:**
- B.Tech/B.E in relevant discipline (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, E&TC)
- 3–8 years experience in railway/infrastructure projects
- GATE qualification or equivalent engineering merit
- Knowledge of railway infrastructure standards and systems
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Railway Economists:**
- M.A/M.Sc in Economics or related discipline
- 3–8 years experience in project planning or economic analysis
- Understanding of transportation economics and project evaluation
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations available)

**Technical Officers:**
- B.Tech/Diploma in relevant engineering discipline
- 2–5 years experience in railway operations or technical roles
- Knowledge of railway systems and maintenance

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.rites.com
- Recruitment cycles: Typically 2–3 per year
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Application fee: ₹200–₹500 (free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Technical Test**
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Topics: Specialized railway/technical knowledge, project management
- Focus: Infrastructure systems, engineering principles, railway standards
- Shortlist: Top performers advance to interview

**Step 3: Technical Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior RITES engineers, project managers
- Discussion: Technical expertise, project experience, problem-solving
- Evaluation: Domain knowledge (50%), experience (30%), communication (20%)

**Step 4: Final Interview & Document Verification**
- Meeting with senior management
- Review of technical qualifications and experience certificates
- Background verification and medical fitness
- Final offer issued
