## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Auxiliary Nurse Midwife (ANM):**
- 10+2 (High School) with science background (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
- ANM certification from recognized nursing school (2-year diploma)
- State nursing registration
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Health Workers (Community Health Volunteers):**
- 10+2 or above education
- Health volunteer training certification (3–6 months recognized program)
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Nursing Assistants:**
- 10+2 education
- 1–2 years nursing assistance experience
- Basic nursing care knowledge
- Age: 18–40 years

**Lab Technicians:**
- 10+2 with science or 2-year lab technician diploma
- 1–2 years laboratory experience
- Knowledge of basic diagnostic procedures
- Age: 18–40 years

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**3-Step Cycle (2–4 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: District/State health department portals
- Application: Online or offline (varies by state)
- Deadline: 15–30 days from notification
- Application fee: Usually none or ₹100–₹200

**Step 2: Written Test (if applicable)**
- Duration: 1–2 hours
- Questions: Basic health knowledge, community health awareness
- Pass mark: 40–50%
- Shortlist: Top performers move to interview

**Step 3: Interview/Selection (15–30 minutes)**
- Panel: District health officers, nursing supervisors
- Topics: Health commitment, patient care, community engagement
- Evaluation: Health knowledge (40%), communication (30%), attitude (30%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Medical Fitness**
- Education certificates verified
- Nursing registration verified
- Medical fitness examination (standard)
- Background check
- Offer letter issued
