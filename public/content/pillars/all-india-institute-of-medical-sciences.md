# AIIMS (All India Institute of Medical Sciences)

## Overview

AIIMS (All India Institute of Medical Sciences) is a premier autonomous institution under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. AIIMS conducts recruitment for medical professionals, nursing staff, administrative positions, and technical roles. This guide covers eligibility, salary structure, selection process, and career opportunities at AIIMS.

## Organization Profile

- **Established**: 1956 (Delhi), with 22 AIIMS institutes across India
- **Status**: Autonomous body, Ministry of Education
- **Workforce**: 5,000+ (medical, nursing, technical, administrative)
- **Main Campuses**: Delhi, Bhopal, Patna, Rishikesh, Raipur, Jodhpur, Nagpur, Hyderabad, Rae Bareli, Bibinagar, Bathinda, Bhaichung, Gorakhpur, Kalyani, Guwahati, Indore, Goa, Madurai, and expanding

## Eligibility Criteria

### For Medical Professionals (Doctors)
- **MBBS/MD/MS** degree from recognized institution
- **Registration** with Medical Council of India (now NMC)
- **Experience**: Varies by position (usually 2-5 years for fellowship)
- **Age**: Generally up to 65 years for senior positions

### For Nursing Staff
- **GNM/B.Sc Nursing** from recognized institution
- **Nursing Council Registration** (State/National)
- **Experience**: 0-2 years for staff nurse, 2+ years for senior positions
- **Age**: 21-40 years (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC)

### For Administrative/Technical
- **Educational Qualification**: Varies by role (12th, Bachelor's, Master's)
- **Age**: 18-40 years (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

- **Sister/Senior Nurse**: ₹45,000–₹65,000 per month

### Administrative/Technical
- **Grade III/IV**: ₹18,000–₹35,000 per month
- **Grade II**: ₹35,000–₹55,000 per month
- **Grade I**: ₹55,000–₹85,000 per month

### Benefits
- Dearness Allowance (DA) - 42% of basic
- House Rent Allowance (HRA) - Location dependent (₹18,000–₹60,000)
- Medical & health insurance for family
- Subsidized housing (if available)
- Pension: Defined Benefit (DB) or National Pension System (NPS)
- Leave encashment & gratuity
- On-campus medical facilities (free)

## Selection Process

### For Medical Professionals
1. **Written Test** (Objective/MCQ) - Subject-specific knowledge
2. **Interview** - Panel interview assessing clinical competency, communication, experience
3. **Document Verification** - Educational credentials, registration certificates
4. **Medical Examination** - Health check-up
5. **Final Selection** - Merit-based ranking

### For Nursing/Technical Staff
1. **Written Examination** - Objective format on subject knowledge and general awareness
2. **Skill Test** (if applicable) - For technical/nursing roles
3. **Personal Interview** - Personality, motivation, communication skills
4. **Document Verification** - Credentials and certificates
5. **Medical Examination**
6. **Final Selection** - Merit list declaration

## Job Opportunities at AIIMS

### Clinical Roles
- Senior Resident (SR) - 3-5 years
- Assistant Professor - 5-7 years
- Associate Professor - 8+ years
- Professor - 12+ years

### Non-Clinical Roles
- Nursing Officer (Staff Nurse, Sister, Senior Nurse)
- Administrative Officers (Finance, HR, Administration)
- Technical Staff (Radiography, Laboratory Technician, etc.)
- IT/Support Staff

## Career Progression

**Medical Professionals:**
- Year 1-2: Senior Resident (learning phase)
- Year 3-7: Assistant Professor (teaching + research)
- Year 8-12: Associate Professor (leadership role)
- Year 13+: Professor/Senior leadership

**Nursing Staff:**
- Year 0-2: Staff Nurse (clinical care)
- Year 3-5: Sister/Senior Nurse (ward leadership)
- Year 6+: Nursing Officer / Supervisor

## Post-Recruitment Benefits

- **Pension**: Defined Benefit (DB) for old employees, NPS for new
- **Housing**: Subsidized quarters (availability varies by location)
- **Medical**: Free treatment at AIIMS for self and family
- **Education**: Children's education allowance (some positions)
- **Transfer**: Postings across AIIMS institutes (every 3-5 years)
- **Research**: Access to world-class facilities and collaboration opportunities
- **International Deputation**: Opportunities for fellowships and exchange programs

## Competitive Analysis

**vs Private Hospitals:**
- AIIMS salary is 15-25% lower than top private hospitals
- But: Job security, pension, housing, work-life balance are superior

**vs Government Medical College:**
- Similar pay and benefits
- AIIMS has better infrastructure and research opportunities

**vs IIT/University Teaching:**
- AIIMS medical faculty salaries are comparable to university professors
- Better work environment for patient care + teaching combination

## Preparation Tips for AIIMS Recruitment

1. **Subject Knowledge**: Keep current with medical advancements and guidelines
2. **Publications**: Write and publish research papers (highly valued)
3. **Teaching Experience**: Gain from current position or previous roles
4. **Communication Skills**: Prepare for technical + non-technical interview questions
5. **Application**: Highlight unique contributions, research interests, specialty skills
6. **References**: Strong recommendations from current mentors/colleagues
7. **Document Readiness**: Keep all certificates, registration documents, publications organized

## Common Interview Questions

- What is your clinical expertise and specialty?
- Tell us about your research/academic contributions
- Why do you want to join AIIMS?
- How do you contribute to medical education?
- What is your vision for advancing healthcare in India?
- Describe a challenging case you managed
- How do you handle work-life balance?
- What are your career goals at AIIMS?
