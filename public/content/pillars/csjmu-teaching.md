# CSJMU Teaching Recruitment Guide

Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University (CSJMU) Kanpur is a major state university recruiting teaching faculty across multiple disciplines. With 20+ positions available and continuous recruitment, CSJMU offers opportunities to shape undergraduate and postgraduate education in North India with government job security.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

CSJMU operates across diverse academic departments, offering positions in:

- **Faculty - Humanities:** English, history, philosophy, sociology, political science
- **Faculty - Social Sciences:** Economics, geography, public administration, social work
- **Faculty - Sciences:** Physics, chemistry, botany, zoology, mathematics
- **Faculty - Commerce/Management:** Accounting, business administration, economics
- **Research & Administrative Roles:** Research associates, institutional planning

CSJMU positions combine teaching responsibility, research opportunity, and government education sector prestige.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- Master's degree (M.A/M.Sc/M.Com) in relevant discipline
- NET (National Eligibility Test) or PhD preferred (varies by position)
- 0–5 years teaching experience (depends on position level)
- For higher positions: Published research or academic credentials
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Associates & Associates:**
- Bachelor's degree or Master's degree in relevant discipline
- Research interest and academic publications preferred
- Age: 18–40 years (relaxations available)

**Important:** CSJMU values candidates with strong academic credentials, teaching potential, and research interest.

## Salary & Benefits Structure

**Faculty (Lecturer/Assistant Professor):**
- ₹55K–₹90K/month
- HRA: 25–30%, DA: 50%+
- Academic performance incentive: Up to 2 months annually
- Research grant: ₹2K–₹5K/month (for research-active faculty)

**Faculty (Associate Professor/Professor):**
- ₹90K–₹160K+/month (depending on experience and rank)
- Higher HRA and DA
- Research support: ₹5K–₹15K/month

**Research Associates:**
- ₹40K–₹70K/month
- Research contingency: ₹1K–₹3K/month

**Benefits (All Positions):**
- Government medical insurance (CGHS)
- Non-contributory pension after 10 years
- Housing assistance / university quarters
- Leave travel concession
- Professional development fund: ₹30K–₹1 lakh annually
- Conference travel support
- Library and research resources access
- Subsidized institutional facilities
- Children's education allowance

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://csjmu.ac.in
- Recruitment: Multiple cycles yearly
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Application fee: Usually ₹200–₹500 (often free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Evaluation of educational credentials and NET/PhD status
- Academic publications reviewed (if available)
- Shortlist: 3–8 per position
- Teaching experience weighted

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Department heads, senior faculty, university administration
- Topics: Academic expertise, teaching philosophy, research interests
- Evaluation: Subject knowledge (40%), teaching ability (30%), communication (20%), fit (10%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Final Selection**
- Educational qualifications verified
- NET/research credentials confirmed
- Experience certificates cross-checked
- Medical fitness clearance
- Offer letter issued

## How to Apply for CSJMU Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://csjmu.ac.in/recruitment or faculty.csjmu.ac.in
2. **Create Account:** Email registration
3. **Select Position:** Choose target faculty role and department
4. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
5. **Upload Documents:**
   - Bachelor's and Master's degree certificates
   - NET certificate (if available)
   - PhD degree certificate (if applicable)
   - Curriculum Vitae with teaching/research experience
   - Published research or academic work samples (if available)
   - Teaching credentials or experience letters
   - Caste certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
6. **Pay Fee:** Usually ₹200–₹500 (often free for SC/ST/PwD)
7. **Submit:** Confirmation via email

## Selection Process

**Teaching & Academic Assessment:**

**Key Evaluation Criteria:**
- Academic credentials (Master's degree quality and marks)
- NET qualification (major advantage if available)
- PhD or research publications (valued but not always mandatory for lecturer level)
- Teaching experience and student feedback
- Research interest and publications
- Communication and presentation skills

**Interview Topics:**
- Your teaching philosophy and classroom approach
- Most important learnings from your academic background
- How you'd engage students in a particular subject
- Approach to curriculum design and course updates
- Research interests and academic goals
- Balancing teaching and research
- Contribution to university academic growth
- Handling diverse student learner types

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your teaching philosophy and key teaching principles"
- "How would you make [subject] engaging for undergraduate students?"
- "Tell us about significant learnings from your academic career"
- "What research interests do you have?"
- "How do you approach student evaluation and feedback?"
- "Describe your experience with online/blended teaching"
- "How would you contribute to academic excellence at CSJMU?"

**Document Verification:**
- Educational qualifications verified with issuing institutions
- NET score verification (if claimed)
- PhD degree verification (if applicable)
- Research publication verification
- Teaching experience cross-checked
- Character verification

## Key Points for Success

- **Academic Credentials:** Strong Master's degree with good marks
- **NET Qualification:** Major advantage for selection
- **Teaching Passion:** Genuine interest in student learning
- **Subject Expertise:** Deep knowledge of disciplinary content
- **Research Interest:** Demonstrated or articulated research goals
- **Communication:** Clear, engaging presentation and explanation ability
- **Continuous Learning:** Commitment to staying current with discipline
