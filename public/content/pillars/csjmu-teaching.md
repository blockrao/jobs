## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- Master's degree (M.A/M.Sc/M.Com) in relevant discipline
- NET (National Eligibility Test) or PhD preferred (varies by position)
- 0–5 years teaching experience (depends on position level)
- For higher positions: Published research or academic credentials
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Associates & Associates:**
- Bachelor's degree or Master's degree in relevant discipline
- Research interest and academic publications preferred
- Age: 18–40 years (relaxations available)

**Important:** CSJMU values candidates with strong academic credentials, teaching potential, and research interest.

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://csjmu.ac.in
- Recruitment: Multiple cycles yearly
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Application fee: Usually ₹200–₹500 (often free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Evaluation of educational credentials and NET/PhD status
- Academic publications reviewed (if available)
- Shortlist: 3–8 per position
- Teaching experience weighted

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Department heads, senior faculty, university administration
- Topics: Academic expertise, teaching philosophy, research interests
- Evaluation: Subject knowledge (40%), teaching ability (30%), communication (20%), fit (10%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Final Selection**
- Educational qualifications verified
- NET/research credentials confirmed
- Experience certificates cross-checked
- Medical fitness clearance
- Offer letter issued

## Selection Process

**Teaching & Academic Assessment:**

**Key Evaluation Criteria:**
- Academic credentials (Master's degree quality and marks)
- NET qualification (major advantage if available)
- PhD or research publications (valued but not always mandatory for lecturer level)
- Teaching experience and student feedback
- Research interest and publications
- Communication and presentation skills

**Interview Topics:**
- Your teaching philosophy and classroom approach
- Most important learnings from your academic background
- How you'd engage students in a particular subject
- Approach to curriculum design and course updates
- Research interests and academic goals
- Balancing teaching and research
- Contribution to university academic growth
- Handling diverse student learner types

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your teaching philosophy and key teaching principles"
- "How would you make [subject] engaging for undergraduate students?"
- "Tell us about significant learnings from your academic career"
- "What research interests do you have?"
- "How do you approach student evaluation and feedback?"
- "Describe your experience with online/blended teaching"
- "How would you contribute to academic excellence at CSJMU?"

**Document Verification:**
- Educational qualifications verified with issuing institutions
- NET score verification (if claimed)
- PhD degree verification (if applicable)
- Research publication verification
- Teaching experience cross-checked
- Character verification
