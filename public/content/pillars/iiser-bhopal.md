# IISER Bhopal Recruitment Guide

Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal is a premier science research institute recruiting scientists and researchers across physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics. With 21+ positions and continuous recruitment, IISER Bhopal offers pure science research focus, government funding, and the opportunity to advance fundamental science in India.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

IISER Bhopal is dedicated to advancing fundamental science through research and education, offering positions in:

- **Scientists & Faculty:** Physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics disciplines
- **Postdoctoral Researchers:** Research scientists pursuing cutting-edge investigations
- **Research Associates:** Laboratory research support and technical roles
- **Technical & IT Staff:** Laboratory managers, data analysis, research computing

IISER Bhopal positions emphasize pure science research excellence and discovery-driven investigations.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty & Senior Scientists:**
- PhD in relevant science discipline (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics)
- 2–5 years postdoctoral research experience
- Published research in high-impact journals (minimum 4–6 papers)
- Strong publication record with citations
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Postdoctoral Researchers:**
- PhD in relevant science discipline
- 0–3 years research experience
- Published research (minimum 2–3 papers)
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Research Associates:**
- M.Sc in relevant discipline with strong research interest
- 1–3 years research experience in laboratory settings
- Age: 18–35 years

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.iiserb.ac.in
- Recruitment throughout the year
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: None

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Evaluation of publication quality and research impact
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position
- High-impact publications prioritized

**Step 3: Research Presentation**
- 30-minute presentation of research area and contributions
- Focus on fundamental science impact and novelty
- Q&A on research methodology and findings

**Step 4: Technical Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior IISER scientists in relevant discipline
- Topics: Research depth, publication impact, research vision
- Discussion of fit with institute research directions

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter issued
- Contract typically 3 years

## How to Apply for IISER Bhopal Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.iiserb.ac.in/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email-based registration
3. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, research details
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - PhD degree certificate
   - Curriculum Vitae with publication list
   - Research statement (600–800 words on research interests and vision)
   - List of all publications with links/DOIs
   - Experience certificates from previous institutions
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
   - Optional: Recommendation letters from senior scientists
5. **Submit:** Online portal submission
6. **Fee:** None

## Selection Process

**Pure Science Research Focus:**

**Publication Quality & Impact:**
- Number of papers in high-impact journals
- Citation count and research impact
- Novelty and fundamental science contribution
- Presentation in top-tier conferences

**Interview Topics:**
- Your most significant research contribution
- Research methodology and experimental design
- Future research vision and innovation potential
- Ability to mentor PhD students and postdocs
- Teaching philosophy for science education
- Interdisciplinary research potential
- Understanding of fundamental science importance

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most impactful research publication"
- "How would you develop a research program at IISER Bhopal?"
- "What's your vision for fundamental science in your field?"
- "Tell us about your research methodology and discoveries"
- "How do you contribute to pure science advancement?"
- "Describe your experience mentoring junior researchers"
- "How would you balance research with teaching?"

**Document Verification:**
- PhD degree verification with issuing institution
- Publication verification in scientific databases
- Citation impact assessment
- Employment history cross-check
- Medical fitness clearance

- **Innovation Potential:** Unique research direction and novel approaches
- **Mentoring Capability:** Experience guiding PhD students and postdocs
- **Science Passion:** Clear articulation of commitment to fundamental research
