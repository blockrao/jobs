Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

20 answers to help you choose your government job path

What are the easiest government jobs in India?
Postal department (₹24K salary, 10K+ annual vacancies, 2-4% selection rate), Police constable (₹30K entry, 50K+ vacancies, 1-3% selection), Railway clerk (₹35K entry, 50K+ vacancies, 0.5-2% selection). "Easiest" = highest vacancy count + lowest competition.
Which exam should I take if I want ₹100K+ salary?
UPSC IAS/IPS (₹180K entry, career ceiling ₹150K+), Defense Services (₹120K+ entry), Finance/Customs (₹100K-₹150K entry). UPSC is most prestigious but 0.08% selection rate (hardest).
Do I need to live in the state to apply for state PSC?
No. State PSCs (Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, UP, Rajasthan, etc.) accept applicants from any state. However, some states may have domicile requirements for certain posts or limited posts.
What's the difference between SSC and UPSC exams?
SSC: Entry ₹21K-₹35K, 8,000+ annual vacancies, 1-2% selection, moderate difficulty. UPSC: Entry ₹56K-₹75K, 200-350 vacancies, 0.08% selection (hardest exam).
How long does it take to prepare for each exam?
Postal/Police: 6-12 months. SSC CHSL: 8-12 months. SSC CGL: 12-18 months. Banking: 4-8 months. Railways: 12-18 months. UPSC: 18-24 months (hardest).
Which department has the best salary growth?
Finance/Customs: Entry ₹50K-₹65K, ceiling ₹150K+ (highest growth). Defense: Entry ₹35K-₹50K, ceiling ₹120K+. UPSC: Entry ₹56K-₹75K, ceiling ₹150K+ (elite).
Can I apply to multiple government exams at once?
Yes. Many candidates apply to SSC, Banking, and state PSC simultaneously. However, exam dates may clash. Banking exams (IBPS) run year-round. SSC exams are seasonal (Jan-Oct).
What's the success/selection rate for each exam?
UPSC: 0.08% (hardest). Finance/Customs: 0.5-1%. IT/Tech: 0.5-1%. Banking: 1-2%. Railways: 0.5-2%. SSC: 1-2%. Health: 1-2%. Police: 1-3%. Postal: 2-4% (easiest).
Do government jobs allow private practice or business?
Rarely. Most government jobs prohibit private practice or business without explicit permission. Teaching and some Health roles allow limited private practice. Defense, Police, Finance are strictly full-time.
What's the pension structure for government jobs?
Defined Benefit Pension: 50% of last drawn salary for life (after 20 years service). Plus family pension, medical benefits, housing assistance, allowances. Gratuity: ₹5L-₹10L+ on retirement.
Are there reserved category quotas in government jobs?
Yes. SC/ST/OBC categories have reserved quotas (15-27% combined). EWS has 10% reservation since 2019. PWD has additional quotas (4%). State PSCs may have additional state-level reservations.
What's the age limit for each government exam?
Postal/Railways/Police: 18-25 years (relaxation for reserved categories). SSC: 18-30 years. Banking: 20-30 years. Defense: 18-21 years. UPSC: 21-32 years. Most exams allow 5-10 years relaxation for SC/ST/OBC.
Can I switch departments after joining?
Yes, after 5-10 years of service. Transfers subject to administrative approval and departmental needs. Inter-state transfers (for state PSC jobs) possible with consent. Most government jobs are posting-based.
What's the posting structure (state vs all-India)?
Central government jobs (SSC, UPSC, Railways, Banking): All-India posting (transferable anywhere). State PSC jobs: State-specific posting (preference for domicile). Some state jobs allow limited inter-state transfer after 5+ years.
How much HRA (House Rent Allowance) do government employees get?
HRA varies by city tier: Tier-1 cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore): 24-27% of salary. Tier-2 cities: 16-20%. Tier-3 cities: 8%. Example: ₹50K salary in Delhi = ₹12K-₹13.5K HRA.
What exam should I take if I'm from a rural/non-English background?
Postal/Railways: Easiest (no heavy English required, vernacular medium options). SSC CHSL: Medium difficulty (basic English). Police/Defense: Physical + written. State PSC: Often accept regional languages. UPSC: English-heavy. Banking: English mandatory.
Are there women-specific government jobs or quotas?
Women have equal opportunities in most government jobs. Additional quotas for women in: Police (often 10-15% in some states), Defense (increasing in recent years), Education (high female participation). Maternity benefits, childcare facilities provided.
What's the interview success rate by exam?
UPSC interview elimination: ~33% (final rank depends heavily on interview). Banking interview elimination: ~50%. SSC interview: ~20% elimination. Railways interview: ~10% elimination. Police: ~10% elimination.
How do I verify government job notifications and stay updated?
Official sources: SSC.nic.in, UPSC.gov.in, IBPS.in, RRB.org, state PSC websites. JobOye aggregates 28 category guides with verified data. Set Google Alerts for recruitment notifications. Follow official departments on Twitter/LinkedIn.
What's the cost of living vs salary comparison by state?
Delhi/Mumbai: Salary ₹56K-₹75K, rent ₹15K-₹25K (expensive). Pune/Bangalore: Salary ₹45K-₹60K, rent ₹10K-₹18K (moderate). Tier-2 cities: Salary ₹30K-₹45K, rent ₹5K-₹10K (affordable). Tier-3: Salary ₹25K-₹35K, rent ₹3K-₹6K (cheapest). HRA + allowances make metro postings viable.
