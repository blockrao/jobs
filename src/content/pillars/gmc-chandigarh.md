# GMC Chandigarh Recruitment Guide

Government Medical College Chandigarh is a premier government medical institution recruiting medical faculty, nursing professionals, and allied health professionals. With 23+ positions and continuous recruitment, GMC Chandigarh offers medical excellence, government prestige, and the opportunity to serve as both clinicians and educators.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

GMC Chandigarh operates as one of North India's leading medical colleges with teaching hospital, offering positions in:

- **Medical Faculty:** All clinical and pre-clinical specialties
- **Nursing Professionals:** B.Sc Nursing, diploma nursing, specialty nursing
- **Allied Health:** Lab technicians, radiographers, physiotherapists, pharmacists
- **Administrative Medical Roles:** Medical records, hospital administration

GMC Chandigarh positions combine clinical expertise with teaching responsibility and government job security.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Medical Faculty:**
- MBBS with MD/MS/DM/MCh specialization
- Medical registration
- 2–8 years clinical experience (depending on position rank)
- Age: 21–50 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Nursing Professionals:**
- B.Sc Nursing or GNM diploma
- Nursing registration
- 0–3 years experience
- Age: 18–40 years (relaxations available)

**Allied Health Professionals:**
- Relevant diploma/degree
- Professional registration where applicable
- 1–3 years experience
- Age: 18–40 years

- HRA: 30–40%, DA: 52%

- Night duty allowance: ₹2K–₹4K

- ₹28K–₹45K/month
- Similar shift allowances

**Allied Health:**
- ₹35K–₹60K/month
- Specialized allowances where applicable

**Benefits (All Positions):**

- Free treatment at GMC hospital
- Housing assistance / government quarters
- Professional development and training
- Conference travel support
- Leave travel concession
- Children's education allowance

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://gmcchandigarh.nic.in
- Applications online or through national portals
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: ₹500–₹1,000 (Free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Exam (if applicable)**
- For some positions: Medical knowledge assessment
- Focus: Latest treatment protocols, clinical guidelines
- Pass mark: 50%+

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior GMC faculty and hospital administration
- Topics: Clinical expertise, teaching ability, patient care
- For nurses: Patient care philosophy and clinical competency
- Evaluation: Medical knowledge (40%), clinical experience (30%), teaching/communication (30%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Medical Fitness**
- Medical degree and registration verified
- Experience certificates cross-checked
- Medical fitness examination
- Background verification
- Offer letter issued

## How to Apply for GMC Chandigarh Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://gmchandigarh.nic.in/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email and mobile registration
3. **Select Position:** Choose target medical role
4. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
5. **Upload Documents:**
   - Medical degree certificates
   - Medical registration certificate
   - Experience certificates from previous institutions
   - Nursing registration (for nursing roles)
   - Teaching credentials (if applicable)
   - Caste certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
6. **Pay Fee:** ₹500–₹1,000 online (SC/ST/PwD: free)
7. **Submit:** Confirmation via email

## Selection Process

**Clinical Expertise & Teaching Assessment:**

**Medical Knowledge:**
- Latest clinical guidelines and treatment protocols
- Differential diagnosis and case management
- Emergency and critical care management
- Medical ethics and patient rights

**Interview Topics:**
- Your most challenging clinical case and management
- Teaching experience and educational philosophy
- Patient care approach and communication style
- Ability to mentor medical students and junior doctors
- Experience with research or academic publications
- Balancing clinical and teaching responsibilities
- Continuing medical education commitment

**For Nursing Roles:**
- Patient care philosophy and bedside manner
- Critical care and emergency response capability
- Nursing supervision and teamwork
- Patient safety and infection control

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe a complex clinical case you managed"
- "How would you approach teaching medical students?"
- "Tell us about your clinical experience in your specialty"
- "What's your approach to patient communication and informed consent?"
- "How do you handle medical errors and learning from mistakes?"
- "Describe your experience with emergency/critical patient care"
- "How would you mentor junior staff in your department?"

**Document Verification:**
- Medical degree verification with issuing institutions
- Medical registration verification with Medical Council
- Experience verification from previous employers
- Medical fitness examination (standard and specialized)
