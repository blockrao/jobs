# IIIT Kota Recruitment Guide

Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Kota is an emerging center for IT education and research, recruiting faculty, researchers, and technical staff across computer science and information technology. With 35+ positions and multiple recruitment cycles, IIIT Kota offers opportunities to shape India's digital future through quality technical education and research.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

IIIT Kota stands at the forefront of IT education in Rajasthan, attracting talented professionals interested in:

- **Faculty Positions:** Computer science, software engineering, data science, cybersecurity departments
- **Research Scientists:** PhD holders pursuing cutting-edge computing research
- **Technical & Support Staff:** System administrators, laboratory managers, IT support team
- **Administrative Staff:** Accounts, HR, and institutional planning roles

IIIT Kota offers a blend of academic excellence, research opportunity, and government job security with the prestige of India's top tech institutions.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD in Computer Science, IT, or related discipline
- 2–5 years postdoctoral or research experience (preferred)
- Published research papers in reputable conferences/journals (minimum 3–5)
- Strong programming expertise and system design knowledge
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Scientist & Postdoctoral:**
- PhD in Computer Science/IT or M.Tech with exceptional research profile
- 0–5 years experience in research or industry
- Published research (minimum 2–3 peer-reviewed papers)
- Proficiency in programming languages and research methodology
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Technical Staff:**
- B.Tech/B.Sc in Computer Science or related field
- Experience: 3–5 years in IT operations, networks, or systems
- Certifications in relevant technologies (CCNA, Linux, etc.)

## Salary & Benefits Structure

**Faculty:**
- **Assistant Professor:** ₹70K–₹100K/month
- **Associate Professor:** ₹100K–₹140K/month
- HRA: 30% (Kota city), DA: 52%
- Research grants: ₹5–₹15 lakh annually
- Performance bonus: Up to 3 months annually

**Research Scientist:**
- ₹45K–₹75K/month (based on experience)
- Research allowance: ₹2K–₹5K/month
- Conference travel support: ₹1–₹2 lakh annually

**Technical & Administrative Staff:**
- ₹35K–₹60K/month
- DA: 50%+, HRA: 20–25%

**Benefits (All Positions):**
- Government medical insurance (CGHS)
- Non-contributory pension after 10 years
- On-campus housing or housing assistance
- Professional development fund
- Library and publication support
- Subsidized canteen and sports facilities
- Annual research travel budget

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification**
- Website: https://www.iiitkota.ac.in
- Recruitment cycles: Usually January–March and July–September
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from publication

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Criteria: PhD credentials, research publications, programming expertise
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position
- Quality of publications and citation impact considered

**Step 3: Technical Interview**
- Format: Online or in-person
- Duration: 60–90 minutes
- Focus: Research methodology, technical expertise, system design

**Step 4: Research Presentation**
- 20-minute presentation of research area and contributions
- Q&A by faculty panel on research depth and relevance
- Evaluation of communication and technical depth

**Step 5: Final Interview & Selection**
- Meeting with director/department head
- Discussion on research vision and teaching philosophy
- Offer letter issued with contract terms

## How to Apply for IIIT Kota Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.iiitkota.ac.in/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email registration with strong password
3. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - PhD degree certificate
   - Curriculum Vitae with publication list
   - Research statement (500–750 words)
   - List of publications with links (if available)
   - Experience certificates from previous institutions
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
5. **Submit:** Online submission generates confirmation
6. **Fee:** Usually none for academic positions

## Selection Process

**Research Presentation & Interview Focus:**

**Technical Interview Topics:**
- Your primary research area and contributions
- Publications and their impact in the field
- Experience with programming languages and frameworks
- System design and scalability expertise
- Teaching philosophy for computer science students
- Vision for research lab development at IIIT Kota

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most significant research contribution in CS/IT"
- "How would you supervise students in a research lab?"
- "What's your approach to teaching software engineering/data science?"
- "How do you stay current with rapidly evolving IT field?"
- "Describe your experience with systems programming/web technologies"
- "Why IIIT Kota and not IIT/private tech company?"

**Research Presentation Evaluation:**
- Clarity of problem formulation
- Technical depth and innovation
- Publication quality and citations
- Potential for future research impact
