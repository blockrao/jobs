# Manipal University Recruitment Guide

Manipal Academy of Higher Education is one of India's leading private universities with multiple campuses, recruiting faculty across engineering, medical sciences, management, and humanities. With 29+ positions available and continuous recruitment, Manipal offers opportunities to shape higher education with modern teaching methodologies and research emphasis.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

Manipal University operates world-class campuses across India and internationally, offering positions in:

- **Engineering Faculty:** Computer science, electronics, mechanical, civil, biotechnology
- **Medical & Health Sciences:** MBBS, nursing, allied health sciences faculty
- **Management & Business:** MBA, business administration faculty
- **Humanities & Social Sciences:** English, history, economics, management studies
- **Research & Technical Roles:** Lab managers, research associates, system administrators

Manipal positions offer exposure to diverse student populations, research opportunities, and institutional support for professional growth.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD or relevant professional qualification (MD/MS for medical faculty, MBA for management)
- 2–5 years teaching/industry experience in the discipline
- Research publications or industry projects (preferred)
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)
- Good communication skills in English

**Research Associates & Lab Managers:**
- M.Tech/M.Sc with relevant specialization
- 1–3 years experience in research/laboratory settings
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Important:** Manipal values candidates with teaching experience, research interest, and commitment to holistic education.

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.manipal.edu/careers
- Recruitment: Multiple cycles yearly (no fixed window)
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from publication
- Application fee: Usually none

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Evaluation of academic credentials and teaching/industry experience
- Research publications reviewed
- Shortlist: 3–8 per position

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Department head, senior faculty, HR representative
- Topics: Teaching philosophy, subject expertise, research interests
- For medical: Clinical experience and patient care approach
- Evaluation: Subject knowledge (40%), teaching ability (30%), communication (20%), fit (10%)

**Step 4: Final Selection & Offer**
- Discussion with department head
- Offer letter issued with contract terms
- Contract period: Typically 1–3 years initially (renewable)

## How to Apply for Manipal University Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.manipal.edu/careers
2. **Create Account:** Email registration
3. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - Degree certificates (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD)
   - Curriculum Vitae with publications and projects
   - Teaching/industry experience certificates
   - Research statement or teaching statement (500 words)
   - List of publications (with links if available)
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
   - Optional: Teaching videos or research overview
5. **Submit:** Online submission
6. **Fee:** Usually none

## Selection Process

**Academic Interview & Evaluation Focus:**

**Teaching Philosophy Assessment:**
- Your approach to student-centered learning
- Use of technology and modern pedagogy
- Diversity and inclusion in classroom
- Assessment and feedback methods
- Balance of theory and practical application

**Interview Topics:**
- Why teaching at Manipal Academy?
- Your most significant research or industry contribution
- Teaching experience and student feedback
- Approach to mentoring students
- Interdisciplinary collaboration interests
- Research plans and publication goals
- Continuous professional development commitment

**For Medical Faculty:**
- Clinical expertise and latest practice updates
- Patient care philosophy
- Experience with clinical training and supervision
- Research in medical field

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your teaching philosophy and classroom management"
- "What's your approach to engaging diverse learners?"
- "Tell us about your research or industry projects"
- "How do you stay current with your discipline?"
- "Describe your experience mentoring students/junior staff"
- "What innovations would you bring to the classroom?"
- "How would you contribute to institutional research goals?"

**Document Verification:**
- All degree certificates verified
- Teaching/employment history cross-checked
- Publication verification in academic databases
- Medical fitness clearance
