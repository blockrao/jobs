# IIT Indore Recruitment Guide: Faculty & Research Positions

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

Indian Institute of Technology Indore is a world-class engineering research institute recruiting faculty, researchers, and technical staff across engineering and applied sciences. With 39+ positions and 39 recruitment cycles, IIT Indore offers cutting-edge research opportunities, competitive salaries, and leadership roles in India's premier technical education system.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD in relevant discipline (Engineering, Sciences)
- 2–5 years postdoctoral or research experience
- Published research/patents (preferred)
- Age: No upper limit (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Scientist & Postdoctoral:**
- PhD or M.Tech with exceptional research profile
- 0–5 years experience
- Published research (at least 2–3 papers)
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

## IIT Indore Salary & Benefits Structure

**Faculty:**
- **Assistant Professor:** ₹70K–₹100K/month
- **Associate Professor:** ₹100K–₹140K/month
- HRA: 30% (Indore city), DA: 52%
- Research grants: ₹5–₹15 lakh annually (assistant prof)
- Performance bonus: Upto 3 months annually

**Research Scientist:**
- ₹45K–₹75K/month (based on experience)
- No HRA (on-campus housing available)

**Benefits:**
- Government medical insurance
- Pension after 10 years
- Housing assistance / campus accommodation
- Professional development fund
- Library and publication support
- Subsidized canteen, sports facilities

## IIT Indore Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification**
- Website: https://www.iiti.ac.in
- Typically: January–March, July–September

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Criteria: PhD credentials, research publications, potential
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior faculty + department head
- Presentation: 20-min research overview
- Q&A: Research vision, teaching philosophy

**Step 4: Document Verification**
- PhD degree, research certificates
- Publication list, patents
- Medical clearance

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter (2-week acceptance window)
- Contract effective date

## How to Apply for IIT Indore Positions

1. **Visit:** https://www.iiti.ac.in/recruitment
2. **Register:** Email account creation
3. **Download Form:** Application form PDF
4. **Fill Details:** Personal, education, research, publication list
5. **Upload Documents:** CV, research statement, degree scans, recommendation letters
6. **Submit:** Email to HR department
7. **Fee:** None (no application fee)

## IIT Indore Selection Process

**Research Presentation:**
- 20–30 minute presentation of PhD research
- Key findings, publications, impact
- Future research direction at IIT Indore
- Q&A on research methodology

**Interview Topics:**
- Research expertise in your field
- Ability to mentor students
- Research publications and citations
- Teaching philosophy and course design
- Vision for departmental growth
- Interdisciplinary collaboration potential
