# IIT Delhi Recruitment Guide 2026: Faculty, Researchers & Staff Positions

## Overview: Indian Institute of Technology Delhi Recruitment

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi) is one of India's premier engineering and research institutions, consistently recruiting faculty, researchers, and administrative staff across engineering, applied sciences, and management disciplines. With 55+ active positions and 47 ongoing recruitment cycles, IIT Delhi offers competitive salaries, world-class research infrastructure, and career growth opportunities for academics and professionals.

**Why apply to IIT Delhi?** IIT graduates and faculty are among India's most sought-after talent. A position at IIT Delhi positions you for leadership roles in academia, research, industry, and policy. The institute offers mentorship from world-leading researchers, publication support, and international collaboration opportunities.

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements for IIT Delhi Positions

Most IIT Delhi positions require a **Master's degree (M.Tech, M.Sc) or PhD**, depending on the role:

**Faculty Positions (Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor):**
- PhD in relevant discipline (Engineering, Sciences, Management)
- 3+ years of postdoctoral or research experience for Assistant Professor roles
- No upper age limit (age relaxation available for SC/ST/OBC candidates)
- Experience in research publications, grants, and teaching preferred

**Research Scientist & Postdoctoral Roles:**
- PhD or M.Tech with exceptional research profile
- 0–5 years postdoctoral experience
- Published research or patents
- Age: Typically 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Technical & Administrative Staff:**
- Bachelor's degree or diploma (depending on role)
- Relevant certifications (IT, HR, Finance)
- Experience in relevant field (3–5 years typical)
- Age: 21–40 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Important:** Relaxations of 5 years (SC), 3 years (ST), 3 years (OBC), and waiver (PwD) are standard across all roles.

## IIT Delhi Salary & Benefits Structure

IIT Delhi offers competitive compensation aligned with **7th Pay Commission scales**:

**Faculty (Assistant Professor to Professor):**
- **Assistant Professor:** ₹70K–₹100K/month (all-in, including DA and allowances)
- **Associate Professor:** ₹100K–₹140K/month
- **Professor:** ₹140K–₹200K+/month
- HRA: 30–40% of basic pay (location-dependent: Delhi metro rates)
- Dearness Allowance (DA): 53% (subject to revision)
- Performance Bonus: Up to 3 months annually

**Research Scientist & Postdoctoral:**
- ₹45K–₹75K/month (based on experience and qualifications)

**Technical & Administrative Staff:**
- ₹25K–₹55K/month (depending on grade and department)

**Benefits (All positions):**
- Government medical insurance (CGHS)
- Pension after 10 years service
- Gratuity (₹50 lakh+ for senior faculty)
- 45 days annual leave + sick leave
- Housing assistance/campus accommodation (limited)
- Professional development fund (faculty)
- Library and publication support
- Subsidized canteen and sports facilities

## IIT Delhi Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step recruitment cycle (typically 4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application (Weeks 1–3)**
- IIT Delhi publishes recruitment notification on official website
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from publication
- How to apply: Online portal at https://recruitment.iitd.ac.in or official announcement

**Step 2: Shortlisting (Week 4–5)**
- Candidates meeting eligibility criteria shortlisted
- Shortlisting criteria: Educational qualification, experience, publications (research roles)
- Typically 5–10 candidates per position shortlisted

**Step 3: Interview (Week 6–8)**
- In-person or video interview (60–90 minutes)
- Faculty panel includes department heads and senior professors
- Research presentation (if applicable)
- Interview evaluates: Subject knowledge, research vision, communication, fit

**Step 4: Document Verification (Week 9)**
- Proof of educational certificates
- Experience certificates
- Caste certificates (if applicable)
- Medical fitness clearance

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer (Week 10–12)**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter issued with 2-week acceptance window
- Contract/appointment letter follows

**Timeline:** Typically January-March and July-September are peak recruitment periods (before academic sessions begin).

## How to Apply for IIT Delhi Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** IIT Delhi recruitment portal (https://recruitment.iitd.ac.in)
2. **Register:** Create account with email and phone
3. **Login:** Access application form for your desired position
4. **Fill Details:** Personal information, education, experience
5. **Upload Documents:** 
   - Resume/CV (PDF, 5MB max)
   - Educational certificates (degree/diploma scans)
   - Experience certificates
   - Caste certificate (if claiming SC/ST/OBC)
   - Published research or patents (links for researchers)
6. **Application Fee:** 
   - ₹500–₹1,000 (General, OBC, EWS)
   - **FREE** for SC/ST/PwD
   - Payment: Online (Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking)
7. **Submit:** Final submission and confirmation email

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- Incomplete education dates or institution names
- CV exceeding 2 pages (keep concise)
- Weak research statement (if researcher role)
- Missing required certificates
- Applying after deadline (no extensions given)

## IIT Delhi Selection Process: What to Expect in Interview

**For Faculty Positions (Teaching + Research):**

**Written Test (if applicable):** 90 minutes, 50 questions on subject fundamentals

**Interview Format:**
- **Duration:** 60–90 minutes
- **Panel:** 5–7 faculty members (department + interdisciplinary)
- **Evaluation criteria:**
  - Subject expertise (40%)
  - Research vision & publications (30%)
  - Teaching philosophy (15%)
  - Communication & presentation (15%)

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your PhD research and key findings"
- "What are your 5-year research goals at IIT Delhi?"
- "How would you design a graduate course in {your field}?"
- "Describe a challenging problem you solved"
- "Why IIT Delhi and not other institutions?"

**Research Presentation (for Senior Roles):**
- 20-minute presentation of research
- 10 minutes Q&A
- Focus: Innovation, impact, feasibility
