# ECHS Recruitment Guide: Healthcare Careers for Ex-Servicemen

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

Ex Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme (ECHS) is a premier healthcare organization for armed forces veterans, consistently recruiting medical officers, nursing professionals, and healthcare support staff across multiple stations. With 54+ active positions and 16 ongoing recruitment cycles, ECHS offers competitive government salaries, pension benefits, and a mission to serve India's ex-servicemen population.

**Why apply to ECHS?** ECHS positions combine healthcare mission work with government benefits. Roles offer medical advancement, fast-track promotions for armed forces veterans, and postings across major Indian cities. As a veteran-focused organization, ECHS values your service background and offers special relaxations for ex-servicemen.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements for ECHS Positions

Most ECHS positions require a **Bachelor's degree (B.Sc Nursing, MBBS, or equivalent)**, depending on the role:

**Medical Officer & Doctor Roles:**
- MBBS or equivalent medical degree (mandatory)
- Medical registration with state/national board
- 1–3 years clinical experience (for senior roles)
- Age: 21–45 years (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Nursing & Allied Health:**
- B.Sc Nursing or nursing diploma (GNM)
- Nursing registration (state nursing council)
- 0–2 years experience (entry-level to mid-career)
- Age: 21–40 years (relaxations available)

**Administrative & Technical Staff:**
- Bachelor's degree (any discipline)
- Relevant certifications (IT, accounts, HR)
- Experience: 3–5 years in healthcare or government settings
- Age: 21–40 years

**Important:** Relaxation of 5 years (SC), 3 years (ST), 3 years (OBC), and waiver (PwD/ex-servicemen) are standard.

## ECHS Salary & Benefits Structure

ECHS offers **7th Pay Commission scales** plus healthcare-specific allowances:

**Medical Officers (MBBS):**
- **Entry Level:** ₹60K–₹90K/month (all-in)
- **Senior:** ₹90K–₹130K/month
- HRA: 30–40% (location-based)
- DA: 50%+ (subject to revision)
- Medical allowance: ₹3K–₹10K (role-dependent)

**Nursing Professionals:**
- ₹45K–₹75K/month (B.Sc Nursing)
- ₹30K–₹50K/month (Diploma Nursing)
- Shift allowance: ₹3K–₹8K
- Night duty allowance: ₹2K–₹5K

**Administrative/Technical:**
- ₹35K–₹65K/month
- No special allowances (standard DA/HRA)

**Benefits (All positions):**
- CGHS medical insurance (comprehensive)
- Pension after 10 years service (non-contributory)
- Gratuity: ₹50 lakh+ (senior roles)
- 45 days annual leave + sick leave
- Housing assistance (limited)
- Free/subsidized treatment at ECHS centers
- Accelerated promotion for ex-servicemen

## ECHS Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step cycle (typically 3–5 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application (Weeks 1–3)**
- Official website: https://www.echs.gov.in or local ECHS center
- Application: Online or offline (depends on recruitment)
- Deadline: 30–45 days from publication
- Application fee: ₹500–₹1,000 (free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Shortlisting (Week 4–5)**
- Eligibility check: Degree + registration validity
- Shortlisting ratio: 5–10 candidates per position
- Criteria: Medical qualification, experience, clinical skills assessment

**Step 3: Interview (Week 6–8)**
- Format: In-person (medical interviews can be rigorous)
- Panel: 5–7 members (medical senior staff + admin)
- Duration: 45–60 minutes
- Topics: Clinical knowledge, patient management, teamwork, fit

**Step 4: Document Verification (Week 9)**
- Degree certificates, registration proofs
- Experience certificates from previous employers
- Medical fitness clearance (can be rigorous for medical roles)
- Character verification

**Step 5: Final Selection (Week 10–12)**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter issued
- Contract period: 1–3 years (renewable)

**Timeline:** Recruitment waves: March–May, August–October.

## How to Apply for ECHS Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.echs.gov.in/recruitment or local ECHS office website
2. **Register:** Create account with email, phone, Aadhaar
3. **Fill Application:** Personal details, educational qualifications, experience
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - Degree certificate (scan)
   - Medical registration proof
   - Experience certificates
   - Caste certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar copy
   - Recent photograph
5. **Application Fee:** ₹500–₹1,000 online payment (Debit/Credit/Net Banking)
6. **Submit & Verify:** Confirmation email with application ID
7. **Download Admit Card:** 1–2 weeks before interview

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- Incorrect registration number (verify with state nursing council)
- Missing experience proofs
- Submitting uncertified degree copies
- Applying past the deadline
- Entering wrong medical specialization

## ECHS Selection Process: Medical Interview Focus

**For Medical Officer Roles:**

**Written Test (if applicable):**
- 90 minutes, 50 questions on medical knowledge
- Focus: Pharmacology, pathology, clinical management
- Pass mark: Typically 40–50%

**Interview Format:**
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Panel:** Chief Medical Officer, Senior Doctor, HR representative
- **Evaluation:** Clinical expertise (50%), communication (30%), temperament (20%)

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most challenging case and how you managed it"
- "Why ECHS and not private practice?"
- "How would you handle ex-servicemen with trauma/PTSD?"
- "What's your approach to healthcare delivery in remote postings?"
- "Describe a time you worked in a team of medical staff"

**Medical Fitness Assessment:**
- ECG, blood pressure, vision, hearing tests
- More rigorous than standard government roles
- Medical history review (pre-employment)

**For Nursing Roles:**
- Written test on nursing practice + clinical scenarios
- Practical demonstration (bed-making, vital signs, injection technique)
- Interview on patient care philosophy
