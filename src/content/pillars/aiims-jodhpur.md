# AIIMS Jodhpur Recruitment Guide

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) Jodhpur is a world-class medical institute recruiting doctors, nursing professionals, and paramedical staff across all medical specialties. With 29+ positions and multiple recruitment cycles, AIIMS Jodhpur offers excellent healthcare mission work, competitive government salaries, and world-class medical training facilities.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

AIIMS Jodhpur is a premier tertiary care medical center offering positions across:

- **Medical Officers & Consultants:** All clinical specialties (surgery, medicine, pediatrics, psychiatry, pathology, etc.)
- **Senior Resident Doctors:** Specialized clinical training roles
- **Nursing Staff:** B.Sc Nursing, General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM), specialized nursing
- **Paramedical Professionals:** Lab technicians, physiotherapists, radiographers, pharmacists
- **Administrative Medical Roles:** Medical records, hospital administration

AIIMS Jodhpur positions combine government prestige, medical advancement, and the opportunity to serve Rajasthan's healthcare needs through state-of-the-art facilities.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Medical Officers & Doctors:**
- MBBS from recognized medical college
- Medical license/registration with Medical Council of India
- 1–5 years clinical experience (depending on position level)
- Specialized degree (MD/MS/DM/MCh) for senior roles
- Age: 21–45 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Nursing Professionals:**
- B.Sc Nursing or GNM (nursing diploma) from recognized institution
- Nursing registration with state/national nursing council
- 0–3 years experience (entry-level to mid-career)
- Age: 21–40 years (relaxations available)

**Paramedical Professionals:**
- Relevant diploma/bachelor's degree (lab tech, radiography, physiotherapy, pharmacy)
- Professional registration where applicable
- Age: 18–40 years (relaxations available)

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (3–5 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://jodhpur.aiims.edu.in
- Applications online or through national portals (UPSC/SSC)
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Application fee: ₹1,000 (General/OBC), Free (SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Exam (if applicable)**
- For some positions: Medical knowledge test (2–3 hours)
- Focus: Clinical knowledge, latest treatment guidelines, medical jurisprudence
- Pass mark: Usually 40–50%

**Step 3: Interview & Clinical Assessment (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior doctors, hospital administration
- For doctors: Clinical case discussion, management approach, ethical scenarios
- For nurses: Patient care philosophy, critical thinking, communication
- Evaluation: Clinical knowledge (40%), communication (30%), attitude (30%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Final Selection**
- Medical degree, registration, experience certificates verified
- Medical fitness examination (standard and rigorous for clinical roles)
- Background check and character verification
- Offer letter issued

## How to Apply for AIIMS Jodhpur Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://jodhpur.aiims.edu.in/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email and mobile registration
3. **Select Position:** Choose your target role
4. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
5. **Upload Documents:**
   - Medical degree certificate (MBBS/nursing diploma/paramedical degree)
   - Medical registration certificate (for doctors/nurses)
   - Experience certificates from previous hospitals/institutions
   - Internship completion certificate (if applicable)
   - Caste certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
6. **Pay Fee:** ₹1,000 online (SC/ST/PwD: free)
7. **Submit & Confirm:** Receive application ID via email

## Selection Process

**Clinical Interview & Assessment Focus:**

**For Medical Officers:**

**Clinical Knowledge Evaluation:**
- Case-based discussions from your specialty
- Latest treatment guidelines and protocols
- Differential diagnosis approach
- Acute emergency management
- Ethical and legal aspects of medical practice

**Interview Topics:**
- Your most challenging clinical case and management
- Why AIIMS and not private practice?
- Experience with critical patient care
- Approach to patient communication and informed consent
- Teamwork in healthcare settings
- Handling medical errors and complications
- Continuing medical education and specialty development

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe a complex case you managed in your training"
- "What's your approach to emergency triage and management?"
- "Tell us about a time you handled a medical-legal issue"
- "How would you approach teaching junior residents?"
- "What's important to you in hospital work vs. private practice?"
- "How do you stay updated with latest medical advances?"

**For Nursing Professionals:**

**Nursing Competency Assessment:**
- Patient care scenarios and appropriate response
- Infection control and safety protocols
- Communication with patients and families
- Critical thinking in patient assessment
- Ethical decision-making in nursing

**Interview Topics:**
- Patient care philosophy and compassion approach
- Handling difficult patients and families
- Critical care experience
- Emergency response capability
- Teamwork with doctors and support staff
- Continuing nursing education commitment

**Medical Fitness:**
- ECG, blood pressure, vision, hearing tests
- More rigorous for clinical roles
- Medical history and medication review
- Pre-employment physical examination
