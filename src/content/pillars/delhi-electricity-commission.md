# Delhi Electricity Commission Recruitment Guide

Delhi Electricity Regulatory Commission (DERC) and allied electricity sector organizations recruit engineers, regulatory specialists, and administrators. With 20+ positions and continuous recruitment, these roles offer expertise in energy policy, power systems, and regulatory frameworks critical to Delhi's electricity supply.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

Delhi's electricity sector offers positions in:

- **Power Systems Engineers:** Transmission, distribution, grid operations
- **Electrical Engineers:** Power generation, substations, supply systems
- **Regulatory & Policy Specialists:** Energy policy, tariff analysis, regulatory compliance
- **Technical Officers:** Power systems maintenance, operations coordination
- **Administrative Roles:** Compliance, legal, institutional management

DERC positions combine technical electricity expertise with regulatory policy impact.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Power Systems & Electrical Engineers:**
- B.Tech/B.E in Electrical/Power Systems Engineering
- 3–8 years experience in power generation, transmission, or distribution
- Knowledge of power systems and electrical standards
- GATE qualification beneficial
- Professional certifications valuable
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Regulatory & Policy Specialists:**
- Bachelor's degree (Engineering, Law, Economics preferred)
- 3–8 years experience in energy policy, regulation, or utilities
- Understanding of power sector regulations
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations available)

**Technical Officers:**
- Diploma in electrical engineering or B.Tech
- 2–5 years experience in power systems operations
- Age: 18–35 years

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: DERC or allied electricity organization websites
- Recruitment: 2–3 cycles yearly
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: ₹200–₹500 (Free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Technical Test**
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Topics: Power systems, electrical engineering, regulatory frameworks
- Pass mark: 40–50%
- Shortlist: Top performers advance to interview

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior power engineers, regulatory officers
- Topics: Technical expertise, power sector knowledge, policy understanding
- Evaluation: Technical knowledge (50%), experience (30%), communication (20%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Medical Fitness**
- Experience certificates verified
- Educational qualifications cross-checked
- Power systems knowledge assessment
- Medical fitness examination
- Background verification
- Offer letter issued

## How to Apply for DERC/Electricity Sector Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** DERC website or electricity organization portal
2. **Create Account:** Email and mobile registration
3. **Select Position:** Choose target power/regulatory role
4. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
5. **Upload Documents:**
   - Bachelor's degree certificate
   - Experience certificates from power sector employers
   - Technical certifications or GATE score
   - Regulatory/policy experience documentation
   - Caste certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
6. **Pay Fee:** ₹200–₹500 online (SC/ST/PwD: free)
7. **Submit & Confirm:** Application ID via email

## Selection Process

**Power Systems & Regulatory Knowledge Focus:**

**Written Test Topics:**
- Power systems engineering fundamentals
- Electrical distribution and transmission
- Power generation and grid operations
- Electricity regulations and tariff structures
- Energy efficiency and conservation
- Power quality and system reliability
- Environmental and safety standards

**Interview Topics:**
- Your experience in power systems or energy policy
- Handling power supply challenges and grid stability
- Knowledge of Delhi's electricity sector
- Regulatory framework understanding
- Energy efficiency initiatives
- Renewable energy integration
- Customer service and complaint handling

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most significant power systems project"
- "How would you approach power supply optimization?"
- "Tell us about challenges in electricity distribution"
- "What's your knowledge of Delhi electricity regulations?"
- "How would you improve power efficiency?"
- "Describe your experience with power systems operations"
- "What's your vision for Delhi's energy future?"

## Key Points for Success

- **Power Systems Knowledge:** Strong command of electrical engineering fundamentals
- **Sector Understanding:** Deep knowledge of power generation, transmission, distribution
- **Regulatory Awareness:** Understanding of electricity regulations and compliance
- **Problem-Solving:** Ability to handle complex power systems issues
- **Technical Expertise:** Proficiency with power systems design and analysis
- **Policy Orientation (for regulatory roles):** Grasp of energy policy and tariff structures
