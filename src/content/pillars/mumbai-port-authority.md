# Mumbai Port Authority Recruitment Guide

Mumbai Port Authority (MPA) is India's largest and busiest port, recruiting engineers, administrative officers, and operations professionals. With 20+ positions and continuous recruitment, MPA offers strategic port infrastructure work, government benefits, and direct impact on India's maritime commerce.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

Mumbai Port Authority operates India's premier port facility, offering positions in:

- **Port Engineers:** Civil, mechanical, electrical, marine engineering
- **Administrative Officers:** Port operations, customs liaison, cargo management
- **Traffic & Cargo Specialists:** Port traffic control, cargo handling coordination
- **Technical & IT Roles:** Port information systems, maritime software
- **Safety & Regulatory Roles:** Port safety, environmental compliance

MPA positions combine infrastructure expertise with maritime commerce focus and government prestige.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Port Engineers:**
- B.Tech/B.E in relevant discipline (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Marine)
- 3–8 years experience in port/maritime/infrastructure projects
- Knowledge of port operations and standards
- GATE qualification beneficial
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD)

**Administrative Officers:**
- B.A/B.Com/B.Sc or MBA
- 3–8 years experience in port operations, customs, or government
- Knowledge of port procedures and maritime regulations
- Age: 21–35 years (relaxations available)

**Traffic & Cargo Specialists:**
- Bachelor's degree (any discipline)
- 2–5 years experience in cargo/maritime operations
- Knowledge of port logistics
- Age: 18–35 years

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**4-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.mumbaiport.gov.in
- Recruitment: 2–3 cycles yearly
- Deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Fee: ₹200–₹500 (Free for SC/ST/PwD)

**Step 2: Written Technical Test**
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Topics: Port operations, maritime regulations, technical knowledge
- For engineers: Civil/mechanical/electrical fundamentals
- Pass mark: 40–50%

**Step 3: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior port engineers, administrative officers
- Topics: Port operations experience, maritime knowledge, technical expertise
- Evaluation: Technical knowledge (40%), experience (30%), communication (20%), port fit (10%)

**Step 4: Document Verification & Medical Fitness**
- Experience certificates verified
- Educational qualifications cross-checked
- Port operations knowledge assessment
- Medical fitness examination
- Background verification
- Offer letter issued

## How to Apply for Mumbai Port Authority Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.mumbaiport.gov.in/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email and mobile registration
3. **Select Position:** Choose target port role
4. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
5. **Upload Documents:**
   - Bachelor's degree certificate
   - Experience certificates from previous employers
   - Port/maritime training or certification
   - Technical qualifications or GATE score (if applicable)
   - Caste certificate (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
6. **Pay Fee:** ₹200–₹500 online (SC/ST/PwD: free)
7. **Submit & Confirm:** Application ID via email

## Selection Process

**Port Operations & Maritime Knowledge Focus:**

**Written Test Topics:**
- Indian port operations and regulations
- Maritime commerce and shipping procedures
- Cargo handling and port logistics
- Environmental and safety standards
- Port infrastructure and maintenance
- Vessel operations and berthing procedures

**Interview Topics:**
- Your experience in port/maritime operations
- Handling complex port logistics issues
- Safety focus and accident prevention
- Knowledge of Indian port development
- Team management in port operations
- Cargo handling and customs coordination
- Port modernization and infrastructure development

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe your most significant port/maritime project"
- "How would you handle a major port congestion issue?"
- "Tell us about cargo handling challenges you've managed"
- "What's your knowledge of Indian port regulations?"
- "How would you improve port efficiency and safety?"
- "Describe your experience with cargo operations"
- "What's your approach to port modernization?"
