# TISS Recruitment Guide

Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS) is India's premier social sciences research and education institution, recruiting faculty, researchers, and professionals across sociology, economics, public policy, management, and social work. With 32+ positions and continuous recruitment cycles, TISS offers opportunities to shape social policy and advance social welfare in India.

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

---

## Overview

TISS is a world-renowned institution for social sciences education and research, offering positions in:

- **Faculty in Social Sciences:** Sociology, economics, political science, anthropology
- **Public Policy & Governance:** Policy analysts, governance researchers, social development specialists
- **Social Work & Community Development:** Community engagement, development program management
- **Management & Business:** Faculty in organizational management, public administration
- **Research & Administrative Roles:** Research coordinators, institutional planning staff

TISS positions offer intellectual engagement, social impact focus, and the opportunity to influence India's social development agenda.

---

## Eligibility & Qualification Requirements

**Faculty Positions:**
- PhD in relevant social science discipline
- 2–5 years postdoctoral or research experience
- Published research in peer-reviewed journals (minimum 3–5 papers)
- Strong research focus on social issues and Indian context
- Age: No upper limit (relaxations for SC/ST/OBC)

**Research Scientists & Fellows:**
- PhD in relevant discipline or exceptional M.A./M.Phil profile
- 0–3 years research experience
- Published work or strong research potential
- Passion for social impact and policy research
- Age: 18–35 years (relaxations available)

**Important:** TISS values candidates with clear social justice commitment, community engagement experience, and research addressing India's development challenges.

## Recruitment Process & Timeline

**5-Step Cycle (4–6 months):**

**Step 1: Notification & Application**
- Website: https://www.tiss.edu
- Multiple recruitment cycles yearly
- Application deadline: 30–45 days from notification
- Application fee: Usually none

**Step 2: Shortlisting**
- Criteria: PhD credentials, research quality, social impact focus
- Shortlist: 5–10 per position
- Publications evaluated on peer-review status and social relevance

**Step 3: Research Presentation**
- 30-minute presentation on research interests and contributions
- Focus on social impact and policy relevance
- Q&A by faculty on research methodology and approach

**Step 4: Interview (60–90 minutes)**
- Panel: Senior TISS faculty, domain experts
- Topics: Research expertise, teaching philosophy, social commitment
- Evaluation: Research depth (40%), communication (30%), social perspective (30%)

**Step 5: Final Selection & Offer**
- Merit ranking finalized
- Offer letter issued
- Contract period: Typically 3 years

## How to Apply for TISS Positions

**Online Application Steps:**

1. **Visit:** https://www.tiss.edu/recruitment
2. **Create Account:** Email-based registration
3. **Fill Application Form:** Personal, educational, professional details
4. **Upload Documents:**
   - PhD degree certificate
   - Curriculum Vitae with publication list
   - Research statement (600–800 words on research area and social impact focus)
   - List of all publications with links
   - Experience certificates from previous institutions
   - Evidence of community engagement or social work (if applicable)
   - Aadhaar/ID copy and photograph
5. **Submit:** Online portal submission
6. **Fee:** None (free for all candidates)

## Selection Process

**Research Presentation & Interview Focus:**

**Social Impact & Policy Relevance:**
- Connection of your research to social development
- Policy implications and recommendations
- Community engagement in research
- Addressing inequality and social justice
- Application to Indian social context

**Interview Topics:**
- Your most significant research contribution to social sciences
- Publications on social issues and policy
- Understanding of Indian social challenges
- Teaching philosophy for social science subjects
- Approach to community-based research
- Experience engaging with marginalized communities
- Vision for social development research at TISS
- Interdisciplinary collaboration potential

**Typical Questions:**
- "Describe a research project that led to social policy change"
- "How do you connect academic research to social practice?"
- "What are India's most pressing social challenges in your field?"
- "Tell us about your community engagement experience"
- "How would you teach social science in a university context?"
- "What's your approach to inclusive and equitable research?"
- "How do you balance academic rigor with social relevance?"

**Key Evaluation Criteria:**
- Quality of social science research
- Policy relevance and real-world application
- Community engagement and grassroots work
- Teaching potential and mentorship capability
- Commitment to social justice and equality
- Interdisciplinary thinking and collaboration
- Understanding of Indian social context
