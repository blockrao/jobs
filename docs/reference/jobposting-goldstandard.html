📋 Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification
  • Degree: Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) or Master of Laws (LL.M.)
  • University: Recognized Indian university
  • Registration: Enrollment with Bar Council of India (mandatory)
Professional Qualification
  • Bar Membership: Must be enrolled with Bar Council of India
Experience Requirements (Multiple Eligible Pathways)
⚠️ IMPORTANT: Candidates must meet ONE of the following conditions. This is not an "OR" list — you need to qualify through one specific pathway.
Eligibility Pathway Experience Required
Pathway 1: State Judicial Service Member 7 years minimum as a member of a State Judicial Service
Pathway 2: State Legal Department 7 years minimum in a superior post in the legal department of a State
Pathway 3: Central Government Legal Affairs 7 years minimum as a Central Government servant with experience in legal affairs
Pathway 4: Academia (Teaching/Research) Master's Degree in Law + 5 years minimum in teaching or research in Law at a university
Pathway 5: Private Legal Practice Qualified legal practitioner with minimum 30 years of age (no specific years mentioned, but practitioner status required)
Source: UPSC Advertisement No. 12/2026 — Official PDF, Page 6–7. Experience eligibility is verified directly from the official notification.

✓ Who Can Apply?

You can apply if you meet ALL of the following:

  • Are enrolled with the Bar Council of India
  • Have at least 5 years of legal practice experience

💼 What You'll Do

  • Draft and examine legislative proposals presented to Parliament
  • Provide legal advice on constitutional and statutory matters
  • Review and analyze complex legal issues affecting government
  • Assist in legal drafting of bills, amendments and regulations
  • Conduct legal research and analysis on government priorities
  • Represent government in legal matters as directed
  • Coordinate with other government departments on legal matters
  • Perform other duties as assigned under service rules
