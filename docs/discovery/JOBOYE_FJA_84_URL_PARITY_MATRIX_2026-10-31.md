## Architecture and publication conclusions

1. **Do not clone broad synonyms.** `Sarkari Job`, `Sarkari Naukri`, and `All India Government Jobs` overlap; consolidate to `/government-jobs` unless independent intent and inventory are demonstrated.
2. **Preserve entity distinction.** `/jobs` lists Recruitment Hubs; `/government-jobs` lists Post Leaves. FJA category parity must not change the canonical Hub/Leaf URL contract.
3. **Sector and qualification are different dimensions.** Engineering Jobs is not equivalent to B.Tech/BE qualification. Teaching, Police, Forest and similar occupations need reliable role/sector classification before new hubs.
4. **State hubs are not ready to scale.** 3,000/3,301 lifecycle-live leaves lack state attribution. DNH and Daman & Diu source URLs map to one merged JobOye UT route; FJA's Puducherry spelling is legacy; Ladakh is in JobOye but absent from the 84-row FJA workbook.
5. **Qualification hubs are low-confidence.** Existing counts are title-regex matches; they can overlap and cannot be presented as verified eligibility. No BA/B.Com/B.Sc/MA/M.Sc/MBA/MSW-specific page should be created from the shared Graduate/Postgraduate patterns.
6. **Updates require their own canonical model.** Results/admit-card/answer-key/corrigendum pages must be based on issuer-backed update records, not a generic Recruitment listing or internal `/reports` dashboard.
7. **No new page family is approved by this matrix.** First complete P0 issuer authority, future-date and historical deadline evidence work; then page-level crawl, search-demand analysis, a single pilot, and route-level release QA.
