This 3-week campaign targets government jobs platforms, education portals, exam prep sites, and career guidance networks with contextual, relevant placements.

- Expected lift: +1,000-3,000 monthly visits

## 3. OUTREACH & FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE

### Initial Outreach
- **Format:** Personalized email (NOT mass mail)
- **Day 1:** Send initial email
- **Timing:** Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM–2 PM IST (best open rates)
- **Personalization:** Mention 1-2 specific sections/pages from target site
- **Call-to-action:** Direct "Would you be open to…?" not vague requests

### Follow-up Sequence
- **Day 4:** If no reply → Send brief follow-up ("Wanted to check in on the previous email")
- **Day 8:** If no reply → Final follow-up with slight angle change (e.g., if guest article didn't work, ask about resources link instead)
- **Day 12:** If no reply → Move to next target; revisit in next outreach wave

### Success Signals to Watch For
1. **Initial Reply:** "We'd be interested" or "Can you send more info?"
   - Action: Provide detailed asset (article draft, link location proposal, etc.)
   - Timeline: 2-3 days max response

2. **Link Placement Confirmation:** "We'll add this to [page/section]"
   - Action: Verify placement within 48 hours; screenshot for tracking
   - Timeline: Check within 1 week of agreement

3. **No Response After Final Follow-up:** Mark as "Unresponsive" and move on

### Status Definitions

- **Pending:** Not yet contacted
- **Sent:** Initial email sent; awaiting reply
- **Replied:** Target replied; negotiating placement
- **Link Placed:** Target agrees to place link; awaiting live verification
- **Confirmed:** Link is live and verified; campaign complete
- **Unresponsive:** No response after final follow-up; deprioritized

### Tracking Best Practices

1. **Daily updates:** Update spreadsheet after each email send/reply
2. **Verification:** Check link placements within 24 hours of "Link Placed" status
3. **Screenshots:** Take proof-of-placement screenshots for all confirmed links
4. **Anchor text monitoring:** Verify anchor text matches agreement (watch for changes)
5. **Backlink monitoring:** Use Ahrefs/SEMrush to track new backlinks weekly starting Nov 20

## 8. RISK MITIGATION

### Common Challenges & Solutions

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low reply rate (<15%) | Personalize emails more; target alternative contacts; adjust subject lines |
| Links not placed after agreement | Follow up within 48 hours; provide link embed code; be flexible on anchor text |
| Links removed after placement | Monitor monthly; maintain relationships; add to "long-term contact" list for future outreach |
| Competitors also targeting same sites | Speed of outreach matters; first-mover advantage; unique value prop (JobOye's UX/data = differentiator) |
| Anchor text changed from agreement | Monitor monthly; note patterns; adjust future negotiations |
| Rankings don't improve despite links | Ensure links are contextual (not footer); verify link quality; wait 4-6 weeks for full impact |

## Appendix: Outreach Contact Research Process

### How to Find Contact Information

1. **Site-specific contact forms:** Check /contact, /about, /submit-article pages
2. **Email patterns:** Check site footer, domain whois, or LinkedIn company page for email format (@domain.com)
3. **LinkedIn:** Search company name + "Editorial" or "Content Manager" to find decision-makers
4. **Wayback Machine:** For outdated contact info, check previous site versions
5. **Site-wide search:** Look for "submit article," "partnership," or "advertise" pages

### Verification Checklist Before Sending
- [ ] Email address is current (test by visiting site recently posted content author)
- [ ] Email is not a no-reply address
- [ ] Subject line mentions specific site content/section
- [ ] Email is personalized (specific person name or "Editorial Team")
- [ ] Call-to-action is clear and specific
