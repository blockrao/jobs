7. 15 FAQ

Q1: Is police work dangerous?

Field duty carries risk. Constable: traffic/patrol = moderate. SI: investigation = higher. Compensation + family pension if injured. Safety protocols improve yearly.

Q2: Can women join police?

Yes. 35% new recruits women (growing). Equal salary, equal authority. 8+ women constable positions. SI/Inspector: merit-based, no gender bar.

Q3: What's the physical test?

5km run in 28 mins (male), 30 mins (female). Push-ups 20+. Height 168cm male, 152cm female. Fitness test: lathi, obstacle course.

Q4: Can I choose posting location?

No initial choice. Government-assigned. After 2-3 years, can request transfer. Home-state posting often granted after service.

Q5: What shift pattern?

24-hour duty rotation typical. 8 hours on, 16 off. Or 2-shift pattern: 6am-2pm + 2pm-10pm. Weekends/holidays working common.

Q6: Do I get OT pay?

Yes. ₹100-₹300/hour. Riot duty, emergency calls get hazard pay. Festival bonuses. Performance bonuses.

Q7: What training required?

6-month police academy (constable), 1-year (SI/Inspector). Firearms, law, investigation, crowd control, first aid. On-job mentoring 6 months.

Q8: Can I do private security?

Limited while serving. Consulting allowed after-hours. Retirement = full private security allowed.

Q9: Is police pension good?

50% last salary for life. Inspector retiring ₹80K = ₹40K/month = ₹12M lifetime. Family pension if killed on duty.

Q10: What's career ceiling?

Commissioner (₹90K+). 30-year growth: Constable → SI → Inspector → DCP → Commissioner.

Q11: Can I refuse posting?

No. Initial posting mandatory. Refusal = dismissal. Later, can request transfer. Hardship postings get extra pay.

Q12: Is police respected?

High community respect. Authority role. Law enforcement prestige. Family respect strong. Pension-backed stability.

Q13: What exam difficulty?

0.8% success (moderate). Written 100 marks. Physical test. Interview. 10th/12th eligible for constable. Graduation for SI.

Q14: Can I study while working?

Yes. Promotion opportunities to SI/Inspector merit-based. Evening study feasible. Career advancement continuous.

Q15: Is police future-proof?

Yes. Law enforcement permanent need. Cannot be retrenched. Pension guaranteed. Technology (CCTV, AI) reduces field risk.
