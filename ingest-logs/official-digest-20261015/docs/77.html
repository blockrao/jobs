RECRUITMENT PORTAL
Recruitment Portal of High Court of Kerala is an exclusive portal to conduct recruitments to various posts in the High Court of Kerala and examinations like The Kerala State Higher Judicial Service Examination and The Kerala Judicial Service Examination. Candidates who desires to apply for the recruitments conducted through the portal should complete a ‘One Time Registration’ to file their application. Also Candidates who have not applied through the Portal on or after 01.02.2024 must complete the ‘One Time Registration’ before filing their application.
