                     Recruitment of Experts for Skill Development Programme in Punjab
NABARD Consultancy Services (NABCONS) is a wholly owned subsidiary of National Bank for
Agriculture & Rural Development (NABARD) and a premium consultancy organization in the field of
livelihoods development, agriculture and rural development in the country (for more details visits
www.nabcons.com) invites online applications from Indian Citizens for various positions in State
Technical Support Agency to Punjab Skill Development Mission.

Punjab Skill Development Mission (PSDM) has assigned NABCONS as the State Technical Support
Agency under Deen Dayal Upadhyay Grameen Kaushal Yojana (DDU-GKY), a flagship programme for
placement linked skill development designed by Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD) and
implemented by PSDM in the state of Punjab. NABCONS is looking for dynamic development
professionals having experience in DDU-GKY Programme for various positions at Chandigarh for
execution of this assignment. Summary of vacancies are given below.

4. Professional Fees
The Consultant will be paid consolidated Professional Fees based on the experience, educational
qualification and overall suitability of the candidate for the above-mentioned post. Salary will be
commensurate based upon experience and last drawn salary. The candidates will be liable for tax
liabilities under section 392 (1) of the Income Tax Act 2025 & Rules in force and the tax will be deducted
at source.

5. Contract Period
The Project Based Contract Staff (PBCS) would be hired for a period till 22 September 2027, which may
be extended based on the requirement of the project and performance or will be co-terminus with
the project period.

6. Travel Allowances
The candidates will be eligible for travel allowances as per the norms of PSDM and will be directly
released by PSDM.
