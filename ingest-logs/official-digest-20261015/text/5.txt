                            a) In case of turnout of candidates is more then 30, the competent Authority at their
                               own discretion shall conduct a screening test to shortlist the candidates for the
                               interview.
                            b) The screening test will consist of multiple-choice question in the concerned
                               discipline and ESIC Related.
                            c) Maximum of three times candidates per vacancy in each category (in order of
                               merit) will be eligible to attend the walk-in interview.
                            d) The performance of the candidate in the interview will be the sole criteria for
                               selecting the candidate for the respective post. Merit participation in the screening
                               test will not confer any claim for the post


                          1. Eligibility Criteria:
                                  I. Age Limit as on 01.10.2026: Not Exceeding 69 Years as on date of Interview for
                                       posts of faculty. However maximum tenure of such engagement will be till 70
                                       Years of the age only.
                                  II. Age Limit as on 01.10.2026: Not Exceeding 45 Years as on date of Interview for
                                       posts of Senior Resident.
                                  III. The upper age limit as on 01.10.2026: Not Exceeding 35 years as of the date of the
                                      interview (age relaxation is applicable as per Government of India rules). For the post of
                                      Junior Resident, the internship must be completed on or before the date of the interview.

                             Note:

                             a) The maximum tenure of such engagement will be till 70 years of age for teaching
                                faculty or 1 (one) year extendable up to 3 (three) years subject to satisfactory performance
                                on contract basis whichever is earlier.
                             b) The tenure of all above posts will be for 01 (One) year extendable up to 03 (Three) Years
                                subject to satisfactory performance on contract basis or attainment of 68 years of age;
                                whichever is earlier.
                             c) These Vacancies are provisional and may increase or decrease as per the actual
                                requirement for the teaching & patient care. The Competent Authority reserves the
                                right of any amendment, Cancellation and changes to this advertisement as a whole or
                                in part without assigning any reason or giving prior notice.
                             d) Reservation will be as per Government of India guidelines issued from time to time.
                             e) All Candidates must have up-dated Medical Registration (NMC/State Medical
                                Council) with all post MBBS/MD/MS Qualification. In case, candidate do not have
                                up-dated registration from Medical Council, they have to show and submit copy of
                                “Applied for document(s)” to concerned authority at the time of Interview. In case,
                                these two clauses are not fulfilled, the candidate either may not be interviewed or
                                their results will remain withheld in case the expert committee allows them to appear
                                at the interview. Even after that, if, candidate selected, they must have to submit
                                updated Medical Registration Certificate on the time of joining.
