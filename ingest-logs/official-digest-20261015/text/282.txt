     Engagement of Additional Legal Counsel & Full time Legal Assistant


      Applications are invited from eligible Advocates interested to serve the
Secunderabad Cantonment Board (S.C.B) as Additional Legal Counsel. The
applicant must possess a Bachelor’s Degree in Law from a recognized
university and must have Bar Counsel enrolment with experience in dealing
and handling of cases in Government Offices under various Rules, Acts and
Regulations. Applicants having knowledge on Cantonment Laws and Rules/
Dealing with Defence land cases within Cantonment area would be given
preference.

      Applications are also invited for fulltime Legal Assistant on monthly
honorarium basis possessing a Bachelor’s de gree in Law from a recognized
university and must have Bar Counsel enrolment with at least 05 years
experience of working independently and handling cases of government
organizations.

      Interested candidates willing to serve in S.C.B are required to submit
their applications along with certificates separately (for the engagement of
Additional Legal Counsel and fulltime Legal Assistant) in respect of their
educational qualification and experience in working as a counsel in any
Government organization, in the Office of the Secunderabad Cantonment
Board, S.P Road, Court Compound, Secunderabad or email to
ceo.scb2009@gmail.com on or before 26-10-2026. CEO reserves the right to
accept/reject any or all applications without assigning any reason.
Application form can be downloaded from https://secunderabad.cantt.gov.in/
