III. PERIOD OF CONTRACT APPOINTMENT:

  1.    The tenure of contract appointment shall initially be for a period of 3 years for
        candidates below 60 years of age and 1 year for candidates above 60 years of age, and
        may be extended further based on performance and the requirement of BMRCL.

  2.    The contract appointment may be terminated by either side by giving 3 months’ notice
        in the case of candidates below 60 years of age and 1 month’s notice in the case of
        candidates above 60 years of age, as applicable.

IV. SELECTION PROCEDURE:
   1.       Candidates who fulfil the prescribed eligibility criteria and are shortlisted shall be
            called for the selection process.
   2.       The selection process shall consist of:
              Physical Fitness Test (PFT)
              Written Test
              Interview

   A. PHYSICAL FITNESS TEST (PFT):

   1.     Candidates shall be required to complete a 1.5 km run within 10 min upto 11.30 min.
          as per their age group. Early finishers will be awarded additional points.

   2.     Candidates shall bring a Medical Fitness Certificate issued by a Government Hospital
          for appearing in the Physical Fitness Test.

         Only candidates who qualify in the Physical Fitness Test shall be permitted to appear
         for the Written Test.

  B. WRITTEN TEST:

   1.    The Written Test shall be conducted after the Physical Fitness Test.

   2.    The Written Test shall be of one-hour duration and shall be conducted in
         Kannada/English language.

   3.    The Written Test shall comprise Essay Writing and General Knowledge.
