                                           Terms of Reference
Tripura Institution for Transformation (TIFT), the State Institution for Transformation of the Government of
Tripura set up under guidance of NITI Aayog, invites applications from eligible Indian citizens for contractual /
consultant / project-linked engagement. The engagements are temporary and do not confer any right to
regular appointments under the Government of Tripura or TIFT.


Nature, tenure and place of engagement
Nature of engagement: Contractual / consultant / project-linked, as decided by the Competent Authority.
Initial tenure: 11 (eleven) months, renewable for further period subject to satisfactory performance, continuing
institutional requirement and approval of the Competent Authority.
Place of engagement: TIFT, Agartala, Tripura, with field travel within or outside the State as required.
The engaged personnel shall work under the supervision and direction of the CEO, TIFT, or an officer
authorized by the CEO, TIFT.
