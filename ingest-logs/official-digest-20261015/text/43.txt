      ENGAGEMENT OF RETIRED GOVERNMENT PERSONNEL ON
                    CONTRACTUAL BASIS
Applications are invited from retired Government officials for engagement on contractual basis
against vacant ministerial posts in the GGSGH, initially for a period of six months or till regular
incumbents join through DSSSB/ Health & Family Welfare, whichever is earlier.

Details of Posts
    Name of Post                        Eligibility                  Nature of Engagement
 Consultant (5 Post)      Retired Government officials from the Purely contractual and
                          posts of Senior Assistant / Assistant temporary
                          Section Officer, up to Section Officer
                          having experience in establishment,
                          administration, accounts, office procedure,
                          file   processing,     drafting,     record
                          management etc.
Terms & Conditions
1. The engagement shall be purely temporary and contractual in nature.
2. The engagement shall initially be for six months or till regular appointment through DSSSB/
Health & Family Welfare, whichever is earlier.
3. The selected retired officials shall not have any claim for regular appointment or
continuation.
4. The remuneration shall be regulated as per prevailing instructions/orders of Finance
Department, GNCD applicable to retired Government servants engaged as consultants.
5. The maximum age limit and other eligibility conditions shall be as per Government
instructions/orders issued from time to time.
6. The competent authority reserves the right to accept or reject any application without
assigning any reason.
7. The applicant should have a clean service record and vigilance Status Report.
8. The number of posts may increase or decrease as per administrative requirement.

How to Apply
Interested retired Government officials may submit their applications in the prescribed format
along with self-attested copies of relevant documents including PPO, Aadhaar Card, PAN
Card, retirement order, educational qualification certificates and experience details.

The completed application form may be submitted in sealed envelope/to;
