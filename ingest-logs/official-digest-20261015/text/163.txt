                 Walk in Interview for Recruitment of Teaching Faculty and Senior Residents
                   on contract basis at ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Surat (Gujarat).



                ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Surat (Gujarat), invites applications from
                eligible candidates to vacant teaching faculty (i.e. Professor, Associate
                Professor & Assistant Professor) positions on a contractual basis for a period
                of 1 years at the outset, with provision of further extension for a period of two
                years based on satisfactory annual performance subject to maximum tenure
                of 3-years or 70 years of age or joining of regular faculty whichever is earlier.


                Further, Applications are invited from eligible candidates to vacant Senior
                Resident on a contractual basis for an initial period of one year, extendable up
                to 3 years subject to requirement and satisfactory annual performance or joining
                of regular incumbent (SR) whichever is earlier.

                A walk-in-interview will be conducted for the month of October-2026 on the 26/10/2026
                and 27/10/2026 (Reporting Time: 09:00 AM to 10:00 AM. No candidate will be allowed
                after 10:00AM) and subsequently on 4th Monday of every month till December 2026.
                If the 4th Monday happens to be a holiday, the interview will instead be held on the
                immediately following next date or as notified from time to time. Notices regarding
                such vacancies shall be published only on the ESIC official website. No separate
                advertisements will be issued in newspapers, will be made available exclusively on the
                official website: www.esic.gov.in/recruitments .


              Reservation & Temporary Engagement: In the event that no suitable or eligible candidate
              from the reserved category is available, the vacancy may be filled through a temporary
              engagement for a period of 44 days. This engagement may be extended by a further 44
              days if required.

                Note:
                1. These vacancies are provisional and may increase or decrease as per the
                actual requirement for the teaching & patient care. The Competent Authority
                reserves the right of any amendment, cancellation and changes to this
                advertisement as a whole or in part without assigning any reason or giving
                prior notice.


                2. Reservation will be as per Government of India guidelines issued from time
                to time.


                3. All Candidates must have up-dated Medical Registration (NMC/ State
                Medical Council) with all post MBBS/MD/MS Qualification(s)). In case,
                candidate(s) do not have up-dated registration from Medical Council, they have
                to show and submit copy of "Applied for document(s)" to concerned authority
                at the time of interview. In case, these two clauses are not fulfilled, the
                candidate either may not be interviewed or their results will remain with-held in
                case the expert committee allows them to appear at the interview. Even after
                that, if, candidate selected, they must submit updated Medical Registration
                Certificate before joining.
                Candidates working in Govt./ Autonomous/ Statutory Institution should
                preferably have "NOC" at the time of Interview from their Employer. In case of
                immediate non- availability of the same, copy of "applied for NOC" and
                undertaking to be submitted at the time of Document Verification before
                allowing interview.
                4. No private practice is allowed in any capacity after joining as teaching
                faculty at ESIC Medical College & Hospital Surat (Gujarat).
                5. Reservation of Person with Benchmark Disability (PwBD) is as per
                prevailing guidelines of Government of India. In case candidates in these
                categories are not available, then the vacancies will be filled from the panel of
                candidates who attend the interview in view of essential services of the
                Medical College and for fulfilment of NMC Norms and reservation of PwBD
                shall be carry forwarded.
                6. The candidate appointed under PwBD quota will be adjusted against
                the vacancy of respective categories of SC/ST/OBC/EWS/General.
                7. Retired/Working Professor may also apply for the eligible posts.

                (F) Terms of Contract:

                i. The tenure of the contract of faculty will be for 1 year at the outset, with provision of
                further extension for a period of Two year subject to satisfactory annual performance,
                willingness and conduct of the candidate and the requirements of this institution. However,
                the contract will be terminated in the case of
                  (a) appointment/joining of a regular faculty on the same post or
                (b) attainment of 70 (seventy) years of age; whichever is earlier between (a) and (b).

                ii. The Tenure of the contract of Senior Resident will be for 1 year at the outset, with
                provision of further extension for a period of Two years subject to satisfactory annual
                performance, willingness, and conduct of the candidate and the requirements of this
                Institution. However, the contract will be terminated in the case of (a) appointment/joining
                of regular incumbent (SR) on the same post or (b) attainment of 50 (Fifty) years of age;
                whichever is earlier between (a) and (b).

                iii. The competent authority reserves the right to terminate the services at any time
                in the public interest, without prior notice or providing any reason. To resign from the
                position, the teaching faculty/SR must submit a one-month advance notice or deposit
                an amount equivalent to one month's salary. Failure to comply with this
                requirement will result in the forfeiture of both the one-month salary and the security
                deposit, and may also lead to the initiation of legal proceedings.

                iv. The teaching faculty/SR shall not be entitled to claim any service benefits, including
                Provident Fund (PF), Pension, Gratuity, Medical Allowance, Medical Benefits, Seniority,
                Promotion, or similar perks.

                v. Provision of Hostel accommodation/ Quarters will be made to the extent possible;
                however, it cannot be claimed as a matter of right. If facility of accommodation is availed,
                no HRA will be payable and the license fee and any other sum in lieu of HRA will be
                deducted as per rule.
