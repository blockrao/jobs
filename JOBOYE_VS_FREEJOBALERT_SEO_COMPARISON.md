# SEO Comparison: JobOye vs FreeJobAlert

**Target**: UP Kasganj Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 – Anganwadi Worker (208 Posts)

## Executive Summary

**Current Gap**: FreeJobAlert ranks for this recruitment; JobOye doesn't appear in similar search results.

**Root Cause**: FreeJobAlert's page has ~8x more structured content, deeper information architecture, and better SEO signals. JobOye's page is minimal (5KB vs 40KB+), lacks critical ranking factors, and is too sparse to compete.

**Opportunity**: JobOye can outrank FreeJobAlert by building a **comprehensively rich job detail page** with full eligibility info, vacancy breakdown, dates, instructions, and FAQs.

## How JobOye Can Rank Higher

1. **Expand "Who Can Apply" Section**
   - Add full eligibility criteria: Gender, Residence, Education, Age, Income/Priorities

   - Show distribution by block/project (Soro: 51, Ganjdundwara: 42, etc.)
   - Allow filtering by location
   - Target searches: "vacancies by location", "how many posts in Kasganj", "block wise vacancies"

3. **Create "How to Apply" Section**
   - 6 step-by-step instructions
   - Link to official portal: upanganwadibharti.in
   - List required documents
   - Target searches: "how to apply", "application process", "what documents needed"

4. **Add "Important Instructions" Section**
   - 6-8 bullet points covering common mistakes, certificate requirements, validation rules
   - Target searches: "application tips", "requirements", "common mistakes"

5. **Expand FAQs to 8-10 Questions**
   - Add: "Is there an application fee?", "How is selection done?", "What are document requirements?"
   - Make them specific, not generic
   - Target searches: every FAQ title

6. **Link to Official Sources**
   - Official Notification PDF
   - Application Portal URL
   - Official Website
